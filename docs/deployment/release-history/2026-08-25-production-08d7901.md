## Why only one of two platforms deployed

Worth recording, because a release where half the pipeline stays quiet looks
like a failure and was not one.

The Render service builds from `rootDir: services/api`. This release changed
five files, all under `apps/admin` and `e2e`, so Render had nothing to rebuild
and skipped. `/api/health` still reporting `2609275` is therefore the **correct**
outcome, not a stalled deploy — and the previous release's record documents the
opposite case, where a served commit lagging `main` was exactly the failure to
look for.

The rule that distinguishes them: check what the *changed paths* imply about
which service should move, before reading a served commit as evidence of
anything.
