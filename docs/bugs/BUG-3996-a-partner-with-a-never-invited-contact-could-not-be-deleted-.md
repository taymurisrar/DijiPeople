# BUG-3996 — A partner with a never-invited contact could not be deleted and the contact could not be removed

## Summary

A contact added through the Contacts tab is stored as a partner portal user with status NOT_INVITED. Delete classified every portal user as blocking, and contacts had no Remove action. One never-invited contact therefore made the partner permanently undeletable. The dialog also read "1 portal users" and told the operator to deactivate portal users, which the Contacts screen never mentions.

## Expected Behavior

- A contact that never activated portal access can be removed, and is deleted together with the partner.
- A contact that has signed in blocks deletion, and the dialog explains why in plain terms.
- Dependency labels are correctly pluralised.

## Actual Behavior

- The `portalUsers` relation was always BLOCKS.
- There was no endpoint or action to delete a contact.
- The admin fallback label lowercased "Portal users", which produced "1 portal users".

## Reproduction

1. Add a contact to a partner.
2. Click Delete.
3. The dialog shows the delete as blocked by "1 portal users", and nothing on the record can remove that contact.

## Evidence

TASK-0037 browser pass, 2026-10-08: screenshot of the delete dialog.

## Root Cause

Two work packages each made a sound decision. WP-07 had portal users block deletion because they can sign in. WP-08 stored plain contacts as portal users with status NOT_INVITED. Nothing reconciled the two decisions.

## Impact

Any partner with a contact could not be deleted.

## Affected Areas

Partner Contacts and partner deletion.

## Proposed Resolution

Remove contacts that never activated, delete them along with the partner, and keep blocking on activated portal users. Pluralise every dependency label.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `DELETE /partners/:id/contacts/:contactId` removes a contact that never activated portal access, and returns 409 for one that did.
- The Remove action is shown only when the API reports `canRemove`.
- Partner delete cascades never-activated contacts inside the same transaction.
- Labels read "1 contact", "2 referral links" and so on.

## Resolution

- New `partner-contacts.ts` holds the rule for which contacts are removable; the filter inside the delete enforces it.
- Deleting a contact revokes its invitation, adds a CONTACT_REMOVED timeline entry and a PARTNER_CONTACT_REMOVED audit entry, and returns `PARTNER_CONTACT_HAS_PORTAL_ACCESS` (409) for contacts that activated access.
- The dependency check splits contacts into those that never activated (CASCADE) and those that did (BLOCKS), and the delete re-counts inside its transaction while holding row locks.
- Labels are pluralised and carry a `countLabel`.
- The admin related-records grid gains a `delete` row command.
