# BUG-4059 — Customer and lead Timeline tabs are always empty

## Summary

The Timeline tab on a customer or lead in platform admin was always empty.

## Expected Behavior

The Timeline lists the lifecycle events recorded for that customer or lead.

## Actual Behavior

Always empty.

## Reproduction

1. Open a customer or lead that has had activity.
2. Open the Timeline tab.
3. Observe no entries.

## Evidence

Reproduced and diagnosed during SESSION-0121 (DP platform remediation) against 4489a3c6. The regression tests named in REG coverage pass with the fix; they were not mutation-tested against the pre-fix code.

## Root Cause

Timeline fell through to the generic AuditLog lookup with entityType Customers and the operator tenant id, but platform lifecycle events are stored under the platform audit routing, so the query matched nothing.

## Impact

Reachable in production through the normal user flow described above.

## Affected Areas

super-admin

## Proposed Resolution

Implemented; see Resolution.

## Acceptance Criteria

The expected behaviour above holds and the regression test passes.

Commit 51c80beb: super-admin platform-lifecycle resolves customer and lead timelines from the platform audit source with the correct entity type; covered by the customer-timeline spec.
