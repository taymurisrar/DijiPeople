# BUG-3332 — Plan cards truncate to eight features so Growth and Enterprise advertise identical capability

## Summary

Each plan card lists at most eight features, cut with `.slice(0, 8)` after
sorting by catalog category order, with no indication that anything was removed.
Growth and Enterprise share their first eight features in that order, so the two
cards render **exactly the same bullet list** while their prices differ by 64%.

Everything that distinguishes Enterprise — Payroll, Compliance & Retention — is
below the cut, as is everything Growth adds over the eight shown. A buyer
comparing the cards has no reason to choose Enterprise, and the checkmarks imply
the list is complete rather than truncated.

## Expected Behavior

A plan card either lists what the plan includes, or says plainly that it is
showing a subset. Where two plans differ, the card shows the difference, since
that is the only thing a comparison is for.

## Actual Behavior

Verified live on 2026-09-11, `dijipeople-demo`, PKR monthly:

| Card | Bullets shown | Features the plan actually has |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 7 | 7 |
| Growth | 8 | 14 |
| Enterprise | 8 | 16 |

Growth and Enterprise both render: Employees, Organization, Leave, Attendance,
Timesheets, Attendance Devices & Gateways, Projects, Recruitment.

The comparison table lower down the page is correct and shows all 16. It is
roughly 1,500px below the cards, and it contradicts them.

## Reproduction

1. Open `/settings/subscription/plans`.
2. Read the Growth and Enterprise bullet lists. They are identical.
3. Scroll to "Included features by plan" and read the Payroll & Finance and
   Platform groups. Enterprise has Payroll and Compliance & Retention; Growth
   does not.

## Evidence

`apps/web/app/(authenticated)/settings/billing/_components/billing-settings-client.tsx:457-475`:

```tsx
plan.features
  .filter((feature) => feature.isEnabled !== false)
  .sort(compareFeatures)
  .slice(0, 8)
  .map((feature) => ( ... ))
```

`compareFeatures` orders by `categoryOrder` then `sortOrder`, both of which come
from the platform feature catalog. The catalog's ordering is Core HR, Workforce
Operations, Work Management, Talent, Platform, Payroll & Finance — so Payroll,
the single most valuable differentiator, sorts last and is always the first thing
cut.

No "and N more" affordance, no link to the comparison table, and no `title`
attribute exists on the truncated list.

## Root Cause

A fixed-height card design met a catalog that grew. `slice(0, 8)` was a layout
decision applied to a list whose ordering happens to put the differentiators at
the end.

## Impact

Reachable in production and directly commercial: the screen understates two of
three plans and makes the most expensive one look identical to the middle one.
Combined with [[BUG-3331]], a buyer who did want Enterprise could not buy it
anyway, but the misrepresentation stands on its own.

## Affected Areas

- `/settings/subscription/plans`
- Any other surface rendering `BillingSettingsClient` plan cards

## Proposed Resolution

No ExecPlan needed.

1. Stop slicing. If the card must stay bounded, show the features this plan adds
   over the plan below it, labelled as such — "Everything in Growth, plus…" — and
   link to the full comparison.
2. If a cap is kept, sort so that the features unique to this plan come first,
   and end the list with a counted, clickable "and N more" that scrolls to the
   comparison table.
3. Use the `isPopular` / `isRecommended` flags the API already returns; no card
   currently carries any emphasis.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No two plan cards render identical feature lists while their feature sets
  differ.
- A truncated list says how many items it omitted and links to the full list.
- Every feature a plan includes is reachable from the card in one interaction.

## Regression Coverage

Needs a REG entry: given two plans whose feature sets differ, the rendered card
lists must differ.
