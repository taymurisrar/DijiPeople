# BUG-4008 — Partner activation reassigns a different partners portal contact by email

## Summary

Activation used `partnerPortalUser.upsert({ where: { email } })` and overwrote `partnerId`. If the email already belonged to another partner's portal contact, activating the second partner silently moved that identity and its future access.

## Expected Behavior

An email-owned portal identity stays attached to its partner. Activation of another partner with the same email is refused before any credential or lifecycle write.

## Actual Behavior

The existing identity's `partnerId`, status and invitation credential were overwritten.

## Reproduction

1. Create or invite a portal contact for partner A.
2. Give partner B the same primary email and make it activation-ready.
3. Activate partner B.
4. Before the fix, the globally unique email upsert reassigned A's identity to B.

## Evidence

`services/api/src/modules/partner-experience/partner-experience.service.ts`, `activatePartner`; regression: `partner-activation.workflow.spec.ts`.

## Root Cause

The email unique key was treated as permission to update identity ownership rather than only as a lookup key.

## Impact

Cross-partner identity ownership and portal authorization can change without approval. This is reachable through duplicate contact emails.

## Affected Areas

Partner Experience activation, partner portal login, Platform Admin partner contacts.

## Proposed Resolution

Look up the global email first, refuse a different `partnerId`, and constrain updates by both identity id and partner id. Covered by EXECPLAN-0055.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A different partner's email yields `PARTNER_CONTACT_EMAIL_IN_USE`.
- The original portal identity and credential are unchanged.
- No email is sent and neither partner lifecycle is changed.

## Regression Coverage

`partner-activation.workflow.spec.ts` — an email owned by another partner is refused without rotation.
