# BUG-4638 — Seat quote serves another customer's reserved plan price to any tenant

## Summary

`GET /billing/plan-prices/:planPriceId/seat-quote` looks a price up by id alone and checks only that it is sellable and in the caller's market. It does not check that the price's plan may be assigned to the caller's customer. A plan reserved for one customer (a negotiated custom plan) therefore had its unit price served to any tenant in the same market that knew or guessed the price id.

## Expected Behavior

A tenant can quote only prices whose plan it may be placed on. A price on a plan reserved for another customer answers exactly as an unknown price does (`BILLING_PLAN_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`), so it also does not confirm the plan exists.

## Actual Behavior

The quote was served (HTTP 200) and contained the other customer's negotiated unit price (7.77 in the test fixture).

## Reproduction

1. Create tenants ALPHA and BETA in one market; give BETA's customer a CUSTOM, SALES_ASSISTED plan reserved to it with a PUBLISHED price in that market.
2. As an ALPHA admin on the web client call `GET /billing/plan-prices/<BETA price id>/seat-quote?seats=3`.
3. Observe 200 and BETA's unit price in the body.

Reproduced 2026-11-22 on the isolated database by `billing-cross-tenant.e2e-spec.ts`; the same suite shows `PlanChangeService` already refused that plan.

## Evidence

- `services/api/src/modules/billing/services/billing.service.ts` `getSeatQuote`: `planPrice.findFirst({ where: { id } })` then `assertPlanPriceCurrentlySellable` and a market check only.
- `services/api/src/modules/billing/services/plan-change.service.ts` already calls `assertPlanAssignableToTenant(..., { opaque: true })` for the same plan.
- Before the fix the regression test failed with status 200; after, it passes (76/76).

## Root Cause

Eligibility lived in `assertPlanAssignableToTenant` and was applied to every way of MOVING onto a plan, but the read-only quote path was never given it.

## Impact

Read-only disclosure of one customer's negotiated price to another tenant. Requires a published price on a custom plan in the caller's market and the price id. No write. Not known to have been exploited. Production was not tested.

## Affected Areas

billing: seat quote endpoint and anything that renders a quote from a price id.

## Proposed Resolution

Apply the shared eligibility rule in `getSeatQuote`, mapping a refusal to the generic unavailable code. Done.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A quote for a price on a plan reserved for another customer is refused with `BILLING_PLAN_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`.
- A tenant can still quote its own reserved plan and any unreserved catalogue plan.

## Resolution

Fixed on branch `agent/dependency-alert-remediation` (SESSION-0137, WP3): `getSeatQuote` calls `assertPlanAssignableToTenant` and converts a refusal to `BILLING_PLAN_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`. A tenant's current plan is exempt inside the helper.
