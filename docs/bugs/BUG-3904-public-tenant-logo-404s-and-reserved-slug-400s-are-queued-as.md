# BUG-3904 — Public tenant logo 404s and reserved-slug 400s are queued as NEW incidents

## Summary

Two expected public-endpoint outcomes are queued in monitoring as NEW incidents:

- `GET /public/tenants/<slug>/assets/logo` returns 404 for a tenant with no
  logo.
- Resolving the reserved slug `dijipeople` returns 400.

Both are correct responses to the request, not faults.

## Expected Behavior

A missing public logo and a reserved-slug rejection are recorded as expected
outcomes, or not recorded at all, and are never queued.

## Actual Behavior

They are queued as NEW incidents.

## Reproduction

1. `GET /api/public/tenants/<slug of a tenant without a logo>/assets/logo`.
   The response is 404.
2. The incident appears as NEW in Platform admin, Monitoring.

## Evidence

- Production `ErrorLog`, read-only query on 2026-10-07: 5 occurrences from
  2026-09-10 to 2026-09-24, 2 in the last 14 days, with no tenant. The slugs
  involved were `demo`, `acme` and `starter` for the logo, and `dijipeople` for
  the reserved slug.

## Root Cause

The expected-outcome classifier does not cover these public routes.

## Impact

Noise in the incident queue. No exposure. The responses do not enumerate
tenants beyond what the public resolve endpoint already returns.

## Affected Areas

- `services/api/src/modules/tenants` (public assets and resolve)
- `services/api/src/modules/error-logs` (the expected-outcome classification)

## Proposed Resolution

Mark these outcomes `NOT_AN_INCIDENT` at classification. Consider returning 204
with no body, or a default asset, for a missing logo.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A missing tenant logo and a reserved-slug rejection create no NEW incident.
