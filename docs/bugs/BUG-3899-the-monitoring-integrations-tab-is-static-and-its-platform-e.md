# BUG-3899 — The monitoring Integrations tab is static and its platform email badge is hard-coded

## Summary

The Integrations tab in Platform admin, Monitoring has no health data at all. It
renders two static cards. The "Platform email" card always shows the badge
"Review provider", whatever the provider's real state.

## Expected Behavior

The tab reflects the real state of each integration, at least Stripe and
platform email, and of the signals the health tiles link to it for.

## Actual Behavior

The Stripe card shows only whether Stripe is configured. The platform email badge
is a hard-coded string.

## Reproduction

1. Configure a working platform email provider.
2. Open Platform admin, Monitoring, Integrations. The email card still reads
   "Review provider".

## Evidence

- `apps/admin/app/(internal)/settings/monitoring/integrations/page.tsx:18`: the
  static badge.
- The page reads only `GET /super-admin/billing/diagnostics`.
- The Email health tile measures **tenant** `EmailDeliveryLog`, while this card
  is about **platform** email. They are separate systems, so the tile and the
  card can disagree even once both are live.

## Root Cause

The tab was shipped as a placeholder and never wired to data.

## Impact

Operators are told to review a provider that may be healthy, and cannot see the
provider state anywhere on the Monitoring screens. Cosmetic and informational
only.

## Affected Areas

- `apps/admin/app/(internal)/settings/monitoring/integrations/page.tsx`
- `services/api/src/modules/platform-communications` (the platform email state)

## Proposed Resolution

Read the real platform-email provider state, and reuse the platform health
payload for the email, queue and storage rows. Label tenant email and platform
email separately.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The email badge reflects the configured platform provider's state.
- The tab shows the email, queue and storage health values from the health
  payload.
- No hard-coded status strings remain on the tab.
