# BUG-3916 — Lead Partner attribution renders above the record tabs on every tab instead of in a tab

## Summary

On a platform-admin lead record, the "Partner attribution" panel (current
partner, partner picker, Reassign, Reason) rendered between the command bar and
the tab strip on every tab. It did not sit inside a tab like Summary or
Commercial. The read-only referral facts it acts on were in a different place
again: Commercial → Acquisition.

## Expected Behavior

Partner attribution is a tab. It shows the referral facts and the audited
reassignment panel together.

## Actual Behavior

`RuntimeRecordEditor` mounted `LeadAttributionPanel` before `<RuntimeForm>`.
The only gate was `moduleKey === "leads" && !isCreate`, with no tab gate.

## Reproduction

Open any lead in admin (for example `/leads/<id>`). The panel appears above the
tabs, and switching tabs never moves or hides it.

## Evidence

- `apps/admin/app/_components/runtime/runtime-record-page.tsx:643-645` at
  `94f65fa4`.
- The panel was introduced in `9f3c3dd5`, a TASK-0032 WP-04 work-in-progress
  checkpoint. No ADR, bug or task chose the placement. The WP-04 report even
  says the panel "appears below the form".

## Root Cause

The panel was mounted in the block of always-on module panels that render
before the tab strip, instead of using the tab-gated mount pattern the admin
record page already provides (Plans Pricing, Contracts Versions).

## Impact

Platform admins, cosmetically. Every lead tab carried an unrelated reassignment
form above its content.

## Affected Areas

The admin lead record page.

## Proposed Resolution

Add a Partner tab and move the referral fields into a "Partner Attribution"
section on it. Mount the panel on that tab only.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Leads have a Partner tab holding the referral fields and the panel.
- The panel renders nowhere else.
- The tab survives even when a direct lead hides every referral field.

## Resolution

- `platform-module-registry.ts`:
  - adds the tabs Summary · Partner · Commercial · Agreements · Activities · System;
  - moves the six referral fields from `acquisition` to `partner-attribution` →
    `partner`. These are `partnerId`, `partnerReferralLinkId`,
    `referralCodeSnapshot`, `referralSource`, `referredAt` and
    `attributionStatus`.
- `runtime-record-page.tsx`:
  - removes the unconditional mount;
  - mounts the panel after `<RuntimeForm>` when `activeTab === "partner"`;
  - names the tab in `hasRuntimePanel` and `hasSpecialPanel`.

Two existing gaps remain unchanged:

- The panel is shown to presales users, who get a 403 when they submit.
- Reassigning while the form has unsaved edits reloads the record.
