# BUG-4052 — Subscribe wizard: Continue on step 4 submits the order from step 5

## Summary

On the public subscribe wizard, pressing Continue on step 4 submitted the order as if the customer had pressed Confirm on step 5. Pressing Enter on steps 1 to 4 also submitted the form past the per-step guards.

## Expected Behavior

Continue only advances to the next step. Only Confirm on the final step submits the order. Enter on an earlier step advances (or validates) and never submits.

## Actual Behavior

Continue on step 4 created the subscription order immediately, skipping the review step.

## Reproduction

1. Open /subscribe and complete steps 1 to 3.
2. On step 4 press Continue (or press Enter in a field).
3. Observe the order request sent before step 5 is shown.

## Evidence

Reproduced and diagnosed during SESSION-0121 (DP platform remediation) against 4489a3c6. The regression tests named in REG coverage pass with the fix; they were not mutation-tested against the pre-fix code.

## Root Cause

apps/landing/app/subscribe/subscribe-form.tsx rendered Continue (type=button) and Confirm (type=submit) from one unkeyed ternary slot. React reused the same DOM button and mutated its type to submit during the click that advanced the step, so the same click submitted the form. Enter on steps 1 to 4 triggered native form submission that bypassed the step guards.

## Impact

Reachable in production through the normal user flow described above.

## Affected Areas

apps/landing

## Proposed Resolution

Implemented; see Resolution.

## Acceptance Criteria

The expected behaviour above holds and the regression test passes.

## Resolution

Commit 51c80beb: Continue and Confirm now have distinct keys so React never mutates one into the other, the submit handler refuses to run unless the wizard is on the final step, and an onboarding submit guard in apps/landing/lib/onboarding-wizard.ts decides whether a submit event may proceed. Guard logic is covered in unit tests.
