# BUG-4007 — Partner activation reports failed delivery as success and cannot retry

## Summary

`activatePartner` marked the partner ACTIVE and rotated its portal credential before checking whether the activation email was delivered. A FAILED or REJECTED delivery still returned success, and the lifecycle guard then refused another Activate attempt.

## Expected Behavior

The partner becomes ACTIVE only after the provider reports `SENT`. A failed delivery leaves the lifecycle and portal credential unchanged and permits a retry.

## Actual Behavior

The old path committed ACTIVE/INVITED first, ignored the delivery result, and stranded the operator after failure.

## Reproduction

1. Put a partner in `INFORMATION_APPROVED` with approved onboarding and a fully signed agreement.
2. Configure `PlatformCommunicationsService.sendEmail` to return `FAILED`.
3. Run Activate, then try Activate again.
4. Before the fix, the first call appeared successful and the second was refused because the partner was already ACTIVE.

## Evidence

`services/api/src/modules/partner-experience/partner-experience.service.ts`, `activatePartner`; regression: `partner-activation.workflow.spec.ts`.

## Root Cause

The database transaction ran before the asynchronous provider outcome and `sendEmail`'s returned status was not inspected.

## Impact

Partner operators can activate a record without delivering the only portal credential. The failure is reachable whenever the configured email provider rejects or fails a send.

## Affected Areas

Partner Experience API, Platform Admin Activate command, partner portal activation.

## Proposed Resolution

Issue a guarded pending credential, send it, compensate on failure, and commit ACTIVE plus audit/timeline/default-link changes only after `SENT`. Covered by EXECPLAN-0055.

## Acceptance Criteria

- FAILED/REJECTED delivery returns `PARTNER_INVITATION_DELIVERY_FAILED`.
- Partner status/account status stay unchanged and the issued credential is revoked.
- Retrying after provider recovery succeeds and audits once.

## Regression Coverage

`partner-activation.workflow.spec.ts` — failed delivery leaves the partner unchanged and permits a successful retry.
