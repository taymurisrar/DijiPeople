# BUG-4019 — Partner record polish: empty application card, clipped subgrid row actions, unfiltered commission lookups, raw ids on commission record

## Summary

The TASK-0037 browser pass found four presentation defects on the Partner record. Each one made a correct record look broken or hard to use.

## Expected Behavior

- Sections with no data are hidden.
- Subgrid row actions stay reachable at every width.
- Lead and customer lookups on a commission offer only that partner's records.
- The commission record names its lead and customer, and shows its status as a label.

## Actual Behavior

- An empty "Application details" card appeared on console-created partners.
- On the Referral Links tab, the Copy, Disable and Regenerate buttons were clipped off the right edge at 1024px and 768px.
- The commission Lead and Customer lookups listed every lead and customer on the platform.
- The commission record showed raw ids, or "Not set", instead of names, and its status as a raw enum value.

## Reproduction

Use the isolated stack:
1. Open a console-created partner and view the Application tab.
2. Resize the Referral Links tab to 768px.
3. Create a commission and open its Lead lookup.
4. Open the commission record.

## Evidence

TASK-0037 browser pass screenshots `80-referral-{1440,1024,768}` and `81-commission-record`, taken before and after the fix.

## Root Cause

- The runtime form had no rule for hiding empty fields.
- The related-records grid had no column for row actions, so they lived inside an overflowing row.
- Runtime lookups could not bind to values on the current record.
- The runtime commission payload carried only the ids it referenced.

## Impact

Low. These are presentation defects, apart from one data-hygiene risk: unscoped lookups make it easy to attribute a commission to another partner's lead.

## Affected Areas

The admin runtime form, related-records panels, runtime lookups, and the commission record and list.

## Proposed Resolution

Fix each defect with a generic runtime mechanism rather than a Partner-specific branch.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Fields marked `hideWhenEmpty` are hidden when their value is empty.
- Row actions are visible at 1440px, 1024px and 768px, with 0px page overflow.
- Lookup endpoints receive `bind.partnerId`, and an unknown placeholder is refused.
- The commission record shows the lead's company and "Paid".

## Resolution

- Field visibility: `hideWhenEmpty` via `field-visibility.ts`.
- Row actions: a sticky row-actions column in `related-records-model.ts`, plus `min-w-0` on the record grid.
- Lookups: `{field}` placeholders sent as `bind.<field>` and validated by the lookups route. The customer list accepts `originatingPartnerId`.
- Labels: `commissionReferenceLabels` adds `leadLabel` and `customerLabel` to every commission payload, and status is now an option field.

## QA Retest

QA-PARTNER-016, browser on the isolated stack:
- Row actions visible 6 of 6 at all three widths, with 0px overflow.
- The application card is hidden.
- The commission Lead shows "Prospect Trading LLC" and the status shows "Paid".
- No console or HTTP errors.
- The first retest showed "Not set" because the isolated API was still a stale process that predated the change. After a restart, it passed.
