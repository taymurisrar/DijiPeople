# BUG-3583 — Counterparty placeholders are grouped under Customer

## Summary

The template placeholder editor's group labels had not caught up with
ADR-0020: `counterparty.*` (always available, on every contract type) was
still labelled "Customer" in the picker, so a partner agreement template's
editor showed a "Customer" group even though ADR-0020 had already removed
`customer.*` from partner-agreement context (BUG-3552/REG-560).

## Expected Behavior

`counterparty.*` should have its own clearly labelled group ("Counterparty"),
distinct from the type-gated `customer.*` group, so a partner agreement
template's editor never implies a customer relationship it cannot have.

## Actual Behavior

`PLACEHOLDER_GROUP_BY_NAMESPACE` mapped the `counterparty` namespace to the
label `'Customer'`. Since `counterparty.*` is always offered regardless of
contract type (ADR-0020), every partner agreement template — which correctly
never offers real `customer.*` fields — still showed a "Customer" group in
its placeholder picker, misdescribing who that group actually refers to.

## Reproduction

1. Open the template editor for a `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` template.
2. Open the placeholder picker.
3. Observe a "Customer" group is offered.

**Live reproduction, throwaway stack, 2026-09-25** (TASK-0032 WP-09 live QA,
"QA agreements DEFECT-4"): the partner agreement template editor listed a
"Customer" placeholder group.

## Evidence

- `services/api/src/modules/contracts/placeholder-context.ts` (or its
  co-located constants) — `PLACEHOLDER_GROUP_BY_NAMESPACE` mapped
  `counterparty` to the literal label `'Customer'`.

## Root Cause

A `doc-code-drift`: the group label was written before ADR-0020 separated
`counterparty.*` (always available) from `customer.*` (context-gated), and
nobody updated the label when the two namespaces were split apart.

## Impact

Cosmetic but actively misleading: an operator authoring a partner agreement
template sees a "Customer" group that has nothing to do with a customer,
which undermines trust in the placeholder groupings generally.

## Affected Areas

- `services/api/src/modules/contracts` (placeholder group labelling,
  `GET /contracts/placeholder-definitions`)

## Proposed Resolution

Give `counterparty` its own `'Counterparty'` group label, ordered right after
`'Platform'`. No ExecPlan needed — a label/grouping fix, no schema or contract
change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `GET /contracts/placeholder-definitions?contractType=PARTNER_AGREEMENT`
  returns a "Counterparty" group (not "Customer"), listed right after
  "Platform".
- No contract type is offered a "Customer" group unless `customer.*` is
  actually in its context.
