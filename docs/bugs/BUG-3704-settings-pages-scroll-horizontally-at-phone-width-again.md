# BUG-3704 — Settings pages scroll horizontally at phone width again

## Summary

At 390 px the Settings shell keeps its configuration side panel beside the page, so every settings page scrolls sideways. [[BUG-1668]] fixed this for tenant workspace pages; the settings shell regressed or was not covered.

## Expected Behavior

No horizontal page scroll at phone width.

## Actual Behavior

`document.documentElement.scrollWidth > innerWidth` on /settings/customization/modules (untouched by TASK-0033) and on the Packages screens.

## Reproduction

1. Open /settings/customization/modules at 390 × 844.
2. The page scrolls horizontally.

## Evidence

TASK-0033 browser pass on the isolated stack at the commit above (pre-existing page measured to separate it from the new screens).

## Root Cause

Not yet established.

## Impact

Every settings page on phones. Not caused by TASK-0033.

## Affected Areas

apps/web settings shell.

## Proposed Resolution

Collapse the configuration panel below the content at small widths; extend the BUG-1668 e2e to settings sub-pages.

## Acceptance Criteria

No horizontal scroll at 390 px on any settings page.

With the fix.
