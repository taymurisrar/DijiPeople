# BUG-0793 — Checkout quotes the alphabetically first plan price currency instead of the visitor market currency

## Summary

`/public/plans` is not market-scoped: it returns every active price on every plan
in every currency any market publishes, ordered by `currency` ascending.
`resolveSubscribeSelection` read `plan.prices[0]` in three branches, so "the
price" meant "whichever currency sorts first" — QAR ahead of USD — and
`subscribe-form` preferred that over the market currency resolved server-side
from published configuration. The home and plans pages read the market currency,
so one visit could show two currencies for one product.

## Expected Behavior

Every public surface quotes the currency of the visitor's market, resolved
server-side from published commercial configuration.

## Actual Behavior

`/` and `/plans` quote the market currency; `/subscribe` quotes whichever
currency sorts first among the plan's prices.

## Reproduction

1. Publish a plan with prices in two currencies whose codes sort differently from
   the market's — QAR and USD, in a USD market.
2. Open `/plans` and note the currency.
3. Open `/subscribe` with no query parameters and note the currency.
4. They disagree, and neither page is obviously the wrong one.

## Evidence

`/api/public/plans` in production returns `availableCurrencies: ['QAR', 'USD']`
with Growth carrying both a QAR and a USD monthly price, QAR listed first
(`orderBy: [{ currency: 'asc' }, ...]` in `BillingService.getPublicPlans`).

- `apps/landing/lib/subscribe-selection.ts` — three unfiltered `prices[0]` / `prices.find` branches.
- `apps/landing/app/subscribe/subscribe-form.tsx` — `const currency = initialSelection.currency || defaultCurrency`, which puts the plan data ahead of the market.
- `apps/landing/lib/plans.ts` — `resolveDisplayCurrency` already documents the market as authoritative, so the two files disagreed about which source wins.

## Root Cause

Authority was inverted. `defaultCurrency` is the market's, resolved server-side;
`initialSelection.currency` is a by-product of list ordering. The `||` preferred
the by-product.

## Impact

A buyer in a market with a local-currency price was quoted one currency while
browsing and another at checkout. Compounded by [[BUG-0792]], which made the
resolved market wrong in the first place.

## Affected Areas

`apps/landing` subscribe wizard, home and plans pages.

## Proposed Resolution

Narrow the selectable prices to the market currency inside
`resolveSubscribeSelection`, pass the market currency in, and make the form read
it directly. A `planPriceId` naming another market's price keeps the plan and
re-resolves the price rather than being honoured or dropped.

## Acceptance Criteria

- With a market currency supplied, the selection's currency is always the market's.
- The price selected is in that currency, not merely labelled with it.
- A `planPriceId` from another market keeps the plan the buyer chose.
- With no market currency — commercial-config failed — every price stays eligible.

## Regression Coverage

`apps/landing/lib/subscribe-selection.spec.ts`,
`describe("resolveSubscribeSelection — market currency")` — seven cases against a
two-currency fixture ordered as the API returns it.

## Dependencies

None.

## Related Items

[[BUG-0792]] — the market-resolution half of the same user report.

## Resolution

`resolveSubscribeSelection` takes the market currency and filters candidate
prices to it; `subscribe-form` reads `defaultCurrency` directly. The home page
also stopped reading `/public/plans` entirely and now renders from commercial
config, so the three surfaces agree by construction rather than by three matching
implementations. Commit `5465697`.
