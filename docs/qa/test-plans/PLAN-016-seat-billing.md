# PLAN-016 — Active-employee billing, usage history and overage

## Scope

`services/api/src/modules/billing/services/active-employee-count.service.ts` and
`seat-usage.service.ts` — the definition of a billable employee, the daily
sampler, usage periods, and overage episodes.

**Deliberately excludes** pricing. What an active employee *costs* is
`commercial-offer.resolver.ts` and PLAN-004; this plan owns only how many there
are and how that is recorded.

## Risks

1. **Counting the wrong population.** Auth users, service accounts or platform
   admins bills for logins, not staff; every Employee row bills for people who
   left. Both are billing incidents, and both are easy to write.
2. **A peak that tracks the latest value**, which silently under-bills a tenant
   that spiked and fell back.
3. **A duplicate daily sample**, which doubles that day in any average.
4. **An import accident billed silently** — 20 capacity to 900 active off a bad
   CSV, invoiced without a human ever seeing it.
5. **A late sample rewriting a closed period** an invoice was produced from.
6. **A missing sample.** A hole in the series cannot be reconstructed later,
   which is why sampleAll never aborts the run on one tenant.

## Preconditions

Real PostgreSQL with migrations through `20260818140000_active_employee_seat_engine`.
At least one `Plan` row (`seed:config`). A tenant with a subscription carrying a
known `purchasedSeats`. No external service.
