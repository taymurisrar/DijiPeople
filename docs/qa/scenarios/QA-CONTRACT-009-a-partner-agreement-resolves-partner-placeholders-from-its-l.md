# QA-CONTRACT-009 — A partner agreement resolves partner.* placeholders from its linked partner

## Preconditions

An individual partner and a company partner, each with a configured
commission and contact details.

## Steps

1. `POST /contracts` `{ contractType: PARTNER_AGREEMENT, partnerId }` for the
   individual partner; preview it.
2. Repeat for the company partner.
3. Manually enter `partner.contact.email` on the individual partner's
   agreement.
4. Rename the partner, then edit the draft agreement.
5. Inspect the individual partner's agreement for a company legal name, a 0%
   commission, an address or a registration number.

## Expected Result

Step 1 stores and prints `partner.name`, `partner.legalName` (the
individual's own name), `partner.contact.*`, `partner.taxId` and the
configured commission. Step 5 finds none of the company-only fields invented.
Step 4's edit refreshes `partner.name` to the new value. Step 3's manually
entered email survives the refresh in step 4.
