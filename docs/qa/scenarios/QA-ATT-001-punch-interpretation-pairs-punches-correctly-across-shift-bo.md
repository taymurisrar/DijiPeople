# QA-ATT-001 — Punch interpretation pairs punches correctly across shift boundaries

## Preconditions

Work patterns including one shift crossing midnight.

## Steps

1. Interpret a normal in/out pair.
2. Interpret a pair crossing midnight.
3. Interpret an unpaired punch, and a duplicate punch at the same instant.

## Expected Result

Each produces the intended worked minutes, and unpaired or duplicate punches are handled explicitly rather than producing a negative or doubled duration.

## Notes

Worked minutes reach payroll. The `workedMinutes` field in `schema.prisma` carries a comment explaining why it is stored rather than derived.
