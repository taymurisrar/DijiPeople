# QA-BILLING-047 — A bank-transfer claim is untrusted and its evidence fails closed

## Preconditions

An approved PKR bank-transfer method, one published self-service price, a buyer
who has completed organization/owner/legal details, and durable object storage
configured for test-owned objects. The scanner can be made READY, UNSAFE,
UNAVAILABLE and PENDING without contacting a production service.

## Steps

1. Submit the order before verifying the administrator email; also try to read
   the invoice and receiving instructions through the order-status route.
2. Verify the email, create the order, and record its server-calculated plan,
   price version, seats, cycle, subtotal, tax, discount, total, currency,
   invoice number, due date and payment reference.
3. Repeat the submission, then post client fields for amount, currency, tax,
   discount, status, tenant, approval, bank account and payment reference.
4. Submit a transaction claim with only a transaction id; then with a receipt
   image; then refresh and resubmit both.
5. Upload an empty file, an oversized file, an executable renamed `.pdf`, a
   MIME/extension mismatch, an allowed PDF/image that scans unsafe, and an
   allowed file while scanning or storage is unavailable.
6. As the buyer, another order holder, an unauthenticated caller, a read-only
   platform role and an authorized verifier, attempt to list/download the
   evidence. Try supplying a storage key directly.
7. Let the order expire, then try to claim payment and upload new evidence.

## Expected Result

- No payable invoice or receiving instructions are disclosed before verified
  email ownership. Unknown and unauthorized order access does not enumerate
  whether an order, account or file exists.
- The server freezes every commercial and money field. Unknown authoritative
  fields are rejected; a repeated valid submission reuses one open order,
  invoice and reference.
- A claim or receipt moves only to `PENDING_VERIFICATION`; it never marks an
  invoice paid, activates a subscription or emits provisioning.
- Evidence is accepted only after boundary size limits, extension/MIME and
  magic-byte agreement, malware scanning and scoped durable storage all pass.
  `PENDING`, `UNSAFE` or unavailable scanning fails closed and never creates a
  usable evidence row.
- The client never chooses or receives `storageKey`. Downloads resolve the key
  from an order-scoped row and are available only to the buyer's scoped status
  capability and an authorized platform verifier. Audit/log/error payloads do
  not contain bytes, full bank details, signed URLs or storage keys.
- Expired/cancelled orders accept no new claim or evidence and cannot be
  revived by a browser retry.
