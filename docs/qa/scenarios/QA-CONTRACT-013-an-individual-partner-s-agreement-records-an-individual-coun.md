# QA-CONTRACT-013 — An individual partner's agreement records an INDIVIDUAL counterparty party

## Preconditions

A platform user holding `contracts.manage`; one ACTIVE partner of type
INDIVIDUAL and one of type COMPANY.

## Steps

1. `POST /contracts` with `partnerId` of the individual partner and no
   explicit `parties`.
2. Repeat for the company partner.
3. Read each agreement's parties.

## Expected Result

The platform party is PLATFORM in both. The counterparty party is INDIVIDUAL
for the individual partner and PARTNER for the company partner; signing and
the signed copy treat both as the counterparty.
