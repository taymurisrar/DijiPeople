# QA-BILLING-049 — Only a verified bank payment can provision, and lifecycle changes never double-charge

## Preconditions

A disposable end-to-end environment with test email, object storage/scanning,
workers and PostgreSQL; one approved test bank account; Starter, Growth and
Enterprise published for PKR monthly/annual purchase; and no production provider
credentials. Every fixture is tagged to the run and cleaned by that run.

## Steps

1. Complete landing signup through legal acceptance, email verification, bank
   order/invoice creation and a transaction claim. Observe the system before an
   operator decision.
2. Reject one claim and request clarification on another. For a third, verify
   independently and approve the exact incoming amount.
3. Retry approval and all outbox consumers; wait for provisioning and sign in to
   the resulting workspace.
4. Confirm the stored plan/version, monthly or annual period, purchased seats,
   entitlements, invoice, payment and customer history.
5. Issue a renewal, leave it unpaid through reminder, grace and expiry, then pay
   it late. Exercise cancel/resume and a plan downgrade.
6. Exercise a plan upgrade and seat increase/decrease, verifying the adjustment
   is either invoiced or scheduled according to the frozen commercial rule.
7. Transition payment method bank → Safepay/Stripe and back using test doubles;
   deliver late callbacks from the old method and duplicate callbacks from the
   new one.
8. Reconcile and refund a verified payment, including retry and failure paths.

## Expected Result

- Before approval there is no tenant, active subscription, provisioning request
  or workspace access. Rejection/clarification and any customer claim remain
  non-activating.
- Exact authorized approval emits the existing payment-confirmed/outbox chain
  once. One customer, tenant, subscription, invoice and successful payment
  remain after retries; provisioning is idempotent and the workspace is usable.
- The activated subscription matches the frozen plan, price version, cycle,
  seats and entitlements for Starter, Growth or Enterprise. A custom plan grants
  only explicitly configured entitlements; no Enterprise Plus is introduced.
- Renewal reminders are idempotent; unpaid renewal moves through grace and
  expiry; late verified payment buys one correct period. Cancel/resume,
  upgrade/downgrade and seat changes preserve paid-through access and create no
  free capacity or duplicate adjustment.
- A payment-method transition has one owner at a time. Late/duplicate callbacks
  cannot charge or credit twice; a real second payment becomes reconciliation
  and refund work.
- Refund and reconciliation preserve audit/history and do not erase the
  original payment evidence. No production transaction or live billing data is
  touched.
