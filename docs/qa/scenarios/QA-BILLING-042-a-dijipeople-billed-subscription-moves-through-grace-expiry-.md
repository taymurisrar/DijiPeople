# QA-BILLING-042 — A DijiPeople-billed subscription moves through grace, expiry and cancel-at-period-end on schedule

## Preconditions

A DijiPeople-billed subscription at each boundary of `periodBoundaryTransition`.

## Steps

1. ACTIVE before its period end.
2. ACTIVE past its period end with `cancelAtPeriodEnd`.
3. ACTIVE past its period end with the renewal unpaid.
4. PAST_DUE inside and past its grace period.
5. A renewal paid while EXPIRED.

## Expected Result

- Nothing happens before the period end.
- Cancel-at-period-end keeps access to the paid-through date, then `CANCELED`.
- An unpaid renewal becomes `PAST_DUE` with `gracePeriodEndsAt` and stays entitled until it passes, then `EXPIRED`.
- A late renewal buys a new period starting at the payment, not days already passed.
- `isSubscriptionLive` treats a Stripe `PAST_DUE` (no grace date) exactly as before.
