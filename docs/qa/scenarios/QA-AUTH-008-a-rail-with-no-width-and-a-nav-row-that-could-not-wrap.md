## Preconditions

A tenant on which the affected surface is reachable, and a signed-in user with
the permissions the surface requires. REG-344 in the regression register carries
the full root cause; this scenario is the reusable check, not the diagnosis.

## Steps

Source-level, no jsdom and no browser available to this task.
