# QA-LANDING-020 — Priority markets lead the country picker ahead of the alphabetical remainder

## Preconditions

A database holding **both** the eight priority markets and the full ISO
country set. This scenario is meaningless against a database with only the
eight defaults — which is exactly the state production is in, and exactly why
the defect was latent there rather than absent.

## Steps

1. Ensure the ISO sync has run, or seed the full country set.
2. `GET /api/public/geography/countries`.
3. Read the first eight entries.
4. Scan the remainder for any name that sorts before its predecessor.
5. Open `/subscribe` and read the top of the Country select.

## Expected Result

- **Step 3** — United States, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Qatar, United Arab
  Emirates, India, United Kingdom, Canada, in that order.
- **Step 4** — no out-of-order transitions. Everything after the pinned eight
  is strictly alphabetical.
- **Step 5** — the picker shows the same order, so "United States" is findable
  at the top rather than between Argentina and Armenia.

The underlying invariant, which is what the unit test asserts: pinned countries
carry a **negative** `sortOrder`, unpinned ones carry `0`, and the two bands
cannot overlap.
