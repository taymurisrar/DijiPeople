# QA-TENANT-050 — Leads are withdrawn rather than bulk deleted

## Preconditions

- Platform Admin, signed in as a `PLATFORM_OWNER`.
- Several leads, at least one of them converted.

## Steps

1. Run `npm --workspace api run test -- bulk-delete-withdrawn`.
2. Open **Leads**, select several rows, and look at the actions available.
3. Open one lead and look for a delete action.
4. `DELETE /api/super-admin/leads` with a list of ids, directly.
5. Delete that single lead from its record page.

## Expected Result

- The suite passes.
- No bulk delete action is offered for a selection.
- Single-record delete is still offered and still works.
- Step 4 finds no such route.

## Notes

Step 3 is the one that catches the wrong fix. The console capability gated
single and bulk deletion together, so the obvious implementation removes both —
and the decision was about deleting *many*, not about deleting at all. A lead
deleted one at a time is a deliberate act on a record somebody has open.

The reason for the decision, worth keeping with the scenario: a lead carries the
attribution a partner commission is calculated from and a partner dispute is
settled with. Converted leads were already refused; the rest are withdrawn rather
than removed.
