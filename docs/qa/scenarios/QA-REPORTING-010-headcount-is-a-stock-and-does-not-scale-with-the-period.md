# QA-REPORTING-010 — Headcount is a stock and does not scale with the period

## Preconditions

A tenant with workforce snapshots covering more than one day, and a workforce that did not change over the range under test.

## Steps

1. Open Workforce Analytics and read Historical headcount over 7 days.
2. Read it again over 30 days.
3. Break it down by department and sum the buckets.
4. Select a period entirely before the tenant's first snapshot.

## Expected Result

The two periods report the same magnitude — headcount is a stock, so a longer window changes which day is reported, never the size of the answer. The department breakdown sums to that headcount, not to employee-days. A period with no snapshot reports nothing rather than zero.

## Notes

The failure signature is a number that scales with the period: 70 over 7 days and 323 over 30 for a company of twelve. Check the **breakdown** as well as the tile — the same defect drawn as a chart is much harder to spot than a wrong number.

Do not assert against the period's end date. The daily job captures `yesterday`, so a period ending today has an empty final day; the metric pins the newest date that exists, and a test written against the period end will pass for the wrong reason or fail for none.

This was unobservable until the workforce backfill ran, because the tile showed an empty state while the table was empty.
