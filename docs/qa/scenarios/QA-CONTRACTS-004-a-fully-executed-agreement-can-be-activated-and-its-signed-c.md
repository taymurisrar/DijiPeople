# QA-CONTRACTS-004 — A fully executed agreement can be activated and its signed copy downloaded

## Preconditions

An agreement whose every required signer has signed (status FULLY_EXECUTED),
with its SIGNED_COPY and EVIDENCE_BUNDLE stored.

## Steps

1. Run the forward transition (Platform Admin "Activate contract").
2. Repeat from FULLY_SIGNED, and from SENT.
3. Open the agreement's Signatures tab and download the signed copy.

## Expected Result

- FULLY_EXECUTED and FULLY_SIGNED become ACTIVE with activatedAt and a
  STAGE_CHANGED timeline entry; SENT is refused and unchanged.
- The Signatures tab lists the signed copy first; it downloads as a PDF.
