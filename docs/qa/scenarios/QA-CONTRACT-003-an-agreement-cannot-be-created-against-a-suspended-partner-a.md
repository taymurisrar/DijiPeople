# QA-CONTRACT-003 — An agreement cannot be created against a suspended partner, archived lead or archived customer

## Preconditions

A `TERMINATED` partner, an `ARCHIVED` lead, an `ARCHIVED` customer, a lead
attributed to partner `p-2`, and an existing non-terminal `PARTNER_AGREEMENT`
for partner `p-1`.

## Steps

1. Create a `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` with `partnerId` pointing at the `TERMINATED`
   partner (repeat for `REJECTED`/`SUSPENDED`/`INACTIVE`).
2. Create an agreement with `relatedLeadId` pointing at the `ARCHIVED` lead
   (repeat for `CLOSED_LOST`/`UNQUALIFIED`/`CONVERTED`).
3. Create an agreement with `customerAccountId` pointing at the `ARCHIVED`
   customer.
4. Create a `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` with `partnerId: p-1` and `relatedLeadId`
   pointing at the lead attributed to `p-2`.
5. Create a second `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` for `p-1` while the existing one is not
   `DECLINED`/`VOIDED`/`SUPERSEDED`/`TERMINATED`/`ARCHIVED`.
6. Repeat steps 1-3 with an `ACTIVE`/`QUALIFIED`/active-status record; repeat
   step 5 via `copy()`, `AMENDMENT` and `RENEWAL`.

## Expected Result

Steps 1-4 each return `400` naming the record and its status (or the
mismatched attribution). Step 5 returns `409` naming the existing agreement's
number. Step 6 succeeds in every case — active-status sources and the
exempt duplication paths are unaffected.
