# QA-AUTHZ-014 — No role-grant route lets an actor grant beyond their own effective access

## Preconditions

An actor holding `roles.assign-permissions` and `SETTINGS:configure` but with no
owner or `SYSTEM_ADMIN` standing. A target user who is not an owner. An editable
role.

## Steps

1. As that actor, attempt to grant `GLOBAL_ADMIN` to the non-owner target
   through `POST /users/:userId/roles`.
2. Attempt the same through `PUT /users/:userId/roles`.
3. Attempt to grant it to themselves.
4. Inspect whether both call sites invoke the shared authority check.

## Expected Result

- Both routes refuse, including the self-grant. The two require the identical
  permission pair, so `PermissionsGuard` treats them as equally sensitive; only
  one used to enforce the rule that makes that pair safe to delegate.
- A spy confirms **both** call sites actually invoke the shared check, rather
  than the outcome merely happening to be right today. Identical decorators
  prove the gate matches, never that the service behind it does.
