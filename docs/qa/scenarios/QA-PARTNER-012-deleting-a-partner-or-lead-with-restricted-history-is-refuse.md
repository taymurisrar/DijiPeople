# QA-PARTNER-012 — Deleting a partner or lead with restricted history is refused by name, not a 500

## Preconditions

A partner with an origin `PartnerInquiry`, an onboarding application, a lead
attributed via `LeadAttributionCorrection`, a lead review and a support case
(one per test case); a partner with none of these; a lead with attribution
history, an agreement, and a `PartnerLeadReview` (one per case); a lead with
none.

## Steps

1. Delete the partner with each restricted relation, one at a time.
2. Delete the partner with none of those relations.
3. Bulk-delete the lead with attribution changes, then with an agreement, then
   with a partner review.
4. Bulk-delete the lead with none of those.

## Expected Result

Each case in step 1 is refused, naming the specific relation. Step 2 deletes
the partner together with its own timeline in one transaction. Each case in
step 3 is refused with a `400` naming the blocking relation(s). Step 4
deletes the lead.
