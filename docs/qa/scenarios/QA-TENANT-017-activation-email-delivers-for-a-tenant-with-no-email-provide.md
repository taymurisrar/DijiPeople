# QA-TENANT-017 — Activation email delivers for a tenant with no email provider of its own

## Preconditions

- A tenant provisioned through the paid public signup, whose owner is still
  `INVITED`. Such a tenant has **no `EmailProviderSetting` row of its own** —
  nothing in provisioning creates one — which is the condition under test.
- Platform email configured and enabled at admin Settings → Email.
- A platform owner session.

## Steps

1. Read `GET /api/platform/tenants/{tenantId}/access` and note the owner's
   `activationEmailStatus`.
2. `POST /api/platform/tenants/{tenantId}/access/{userId}/resend-invitation`.
3. Read the response body's `success`, `delivered` and `deliveryStatus`
   **separately**. Do not infer delivery from `success`, and do not judge it by
   the toast — that is precisely the defect BUG-1515 recorded.
4. Re-read the access endpoint.
5. Open the activation link from the delivered email, set a password, and
   submit.
6. Sign in at `POST /api/auth/login` against the tenant.

## Expected Result

- The resend returns `delivered: true` and `deliveryStatus: SENT`.
- The access endpoint reports `activationEmailStatus: SENT`,
  `activationEmailDelivered: true`, and no `activationEmailDetail`.
- The email arrives, and its link opens an activation page naming the right
  person, work email and workspace.
- After activation the owner is `ACTIVE`, `invitationStatus: Activated`,
  `credentialRotatedAt` set and `activeOwnerCount: 1`.
- The owner can sign in and reaches their own workspace, seeing **only** their
  tenant's data.

## Notes

**A better retest path than the one in Steps.** The tenant workspace has its own
delivery log at
`/settings/notifications/delivery/delivery-logs`, which shows the outcome
without any platform credentials at all. Verified there on 2026-08-27 for
`dijipeople-demo`:

```
Activate your DijiPeople account for DijiPeople Demo
taimurisrar806@gmail.com     SENT
<f48dee24-…@dijipeople.com>  08/27/2026
```

The message id is on `@dijipeople.com` — the platform sender carrying
tenant-scoped mail, which is PLAN-023 working exactly as designed and is the
single clearest piece of evidence for it.

Note also `/settings/notifications/providers` — "Configure tenant sender
identities and provider settings" — so a tenant that wants its own relay has a
screen for it. The platform provider is a default, not a ceiling.


Run against production on 2026-08-27 after `5762b2b2`, on tenant
`f959c5ff-c8f2-419b-ae79-e99989557771`. Every step above passed, including a
real email arriving and the owner signing in; the employee list came back empty
for the new tenant, with no leakage from any other.

The same scenario against `2eadac97` and earlier returns
`activationEmailStatus: FAILED` with
`"No enabled email provider is configured."` — that is the regression this
guards, and it is what made every paid signup provision a workspace nobody
could enter.

Partially automated. `platform-email-provider.resolver.spec.ts` covers provider
selection and the origin routing; **delivery itself cannot be asserted from a
unit test** and needs this manual run, because the failure modes that remain are
credential decryption and the relay accepting the message.
