# QA-PLATFORM-051 — Partner onboarding invitation sends, resends and fails safely

## Preconditions

A platform admin, plus partners in ONBOARDING_PENDING, ACTIVE and SUSPENDED, each with a contact email.

## Steps

1. In services/api, run `npx jest src/modules/partner-experience/partner-onboarding-invitation.spec.ts`, and the DB-backed `test/partner-onboarding-invitation.e2e-spec.ts` against a disposable database.
2. Browser: send the onboarding link and read the notice.
3. Browser: resend it immediately, then resend again after 60 seconds, and open the old link.

## Expected Result

- Both specs pass.
- The notice names the recipient and the expiry date.
- An immediate resend is refused with the cooldown message.
- A later resend delivers a new link, and the old link reports that it is not valid.
- The ACTIVE and SUSPENDED partners are refused with a domain message.
- No token appears in any response.
