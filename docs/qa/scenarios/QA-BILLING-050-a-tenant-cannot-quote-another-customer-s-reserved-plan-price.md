# QA-BILLING-050 — A tenant cannot quote another customer's reserved plan price

## Preconditions

An isolated database with two tenants in one market. BETA's customer has a CUSTOM plan reserved to it with a PUBLISHED price in that market. ALPHA has an unreserved plan.

## Steps

1. As ALPHA's admin call `GET /api/billing/plan-prices/<BETA reserved price id>/seat-quote?seats=3`.
2. As BETA's admin call the same route for the same price (control).

## Expected Result

Step 1 is refused with `BILLING_PLAN_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` and the body contains no BETA unit price. Step 2 returns the quote.
