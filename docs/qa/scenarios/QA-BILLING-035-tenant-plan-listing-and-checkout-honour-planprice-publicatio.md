# QA-BILLING-035 — Tenant plan listing and checkout honour PlanPrice publication and market scope

## Preconditions

A PUBLISHED, active `Plan` with at least two `PlanPrice` rows: one PUBLISHED
and scoped to Market A, one either DRAFT, unscoped (`marketId: null`), or
scoped to Market B. A tenant whose current subscription (or, absent one, its
provisioning `SubscriptionOrder`) resolves to Market A.

## Steps

1. As the tenant, call `GET /billing/plans` and read the returned plan's
   `prices` array.
2. As an anonymous caller, call `GET /public/plans` and read the same plan's
   `prices` array.
3. As the tenant, call `POST /billing/checkout-sessions` naming the DRAFT
   price's id.
4. Repeat step 3 naming the unscoped price's id.
5. Repeat step 3 naming the Market B price's id.
6. Repeat step 3 naming the Market A PUBLISHED price's id.
7. Feed the same fixtures to `resolveCommercialOffer` directly (the resolver
   `/public/commercial-config` uses) and compare its `available` verdict per
   price against step 1-2's listing.

## Expected Result

- Step 1: only the Market A PUBLISHED price appears.
- Step 2: the DRAFT price is absent (both callers), but the Market B price
  may still appear — the anonymous listing is not market-scoped.
- Steps 3-5: each request is refused with `BILLING_PLAN_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`,
  and the three refusals are indistinguishable from each other (no hint about
  which precondition failed).
- Step 6: the checkout session is created.
- Step 7: `resolveCommercialOffer`'s verdict for each price matches whether
  it appeared in step 1's tenant listing — the two paths never disagree about
  the same row.
