# QA-PLATFORM-002 - Platform Admin screens carry no critical or serious accessibility violation

## Preconditions

A platform session, a running admin app and API, and a disposable database. This
half of the suite is gated on the full `probeEnvironment` for exactly that
reason - unlike the public audit, it must sign in and read recorded runs.

## Steps

1. Sign in to Platform Admin as a platform user.
2. Open the provisioning queue and the dashboard.
3. Run axe with the `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a` and `wcag21aa` rule sets and
   filter to `critical` and `serious` impact.
4. At 390, 768 and 1366 pixels, check the page body does not scroll sideways.
5. Confirm the queue's column headers carry `scope`, the table has a caption,
   and every state cell carries text rather than resting on its colour.

## Expected Result

No critical or serious violation on either screen. No horizontal body scroll at
any width. The table is announced with headers and a name, and each of the six
operational states is readable without colour vision.

## Notes

Steps 3 and 4 must both hold, and that pairing is deliberate. The scroll
container exists so a wide table does not drag the navigation shell off-screen
(step 4); making it scrollable created a region only a pointer could operate
(step 3, `scrollable-region-focusable`). Checking one without the other lets a
fix for either be traded against the other - which is how [[BUG-0074]] arrived
in the first place, as the by-product of a correct layout fix.

Step 5 is a hand-written structural check and is kept **alongside** axe rather
than instead of it. On its own it passed while the screen was still
keyboard-unusable: it asserted what its author thought to look for, and axe
asserted what the rule set knows to look for. Both, or neither is trustworthy.

Moderate and minor violations are reported rather than gated. A first audit of a
codebase that has never had one surfaces a long tail; failing on all of it at
once produces a suite nobody can act on, which is worse than no suite.

First run on 2026-08-19 found two real defects - [[BUG-0073]] and [[BUG-0074]] -
one of them in the shared shell and therefore on every admin screen.
