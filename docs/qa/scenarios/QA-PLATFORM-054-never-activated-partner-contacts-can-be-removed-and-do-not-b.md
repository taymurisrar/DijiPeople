# QA-PLATFORM-054 — Never-activated partner contacts can be removed and do not block partner deletion

## Preconditions

A platform admin, plus a partner with one contact that was never invited.

## Steps

1. In services/api, run `npx jest src/modules/partners/partner-contact-removal.spec.ts src/modules/partners/partner-dependencies.spec.ts`.
2. Browser: open Delete on the partner and read the dialog.
3. Browser: remove the contact from the Contacts tab, or delete the partner directly.

## Expected Result

- Both specs pass.
- The dialog lists "1 contact" under will also be deleted, and nothing blocks the delete.
- The delete succeeds and takes the contact with it.
- A contact that has activated portal access blocks the delete with a plain-language reason.
