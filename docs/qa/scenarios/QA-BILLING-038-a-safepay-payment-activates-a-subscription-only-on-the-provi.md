# QA-BILLING-038 — A Safepay payment activates a subscription only on the provider's confirmation of the exact amount and currency

## Preconditions

A tenant with an `INCOMPLETE` Safepay-billed subscription, an `ISSUED` invoice whose `metadataJson.kind` is `INITIAL`, and a `PENDING` payment carrying a Safepay tracker.

## Steps

1. Have the provider report the tracker PENDING and settle.
2. Report it terminally failed and settle.
3. Report it paid for a different amount, then a different currency.
4. Report it paid for exactly the invoice amount and currency.

## Expected Result

- PENDING changes nothing.
- A terminal failure marks the payment `FAILED` and the invoice `PAYMENT_FAILED`; the subscription stays `INCOMPLETE`.
- A mismatch is never credited: payment `FAILED` with `SAFEPAY_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` / `SAFEPAY_CURRENCY_MISMATCH` and a CRITICAL platform event.
- An exact match makes the payment `SUCCEEDED`, the invoice `PAID` and the subscription `ACTIVE` for one billing interval.
