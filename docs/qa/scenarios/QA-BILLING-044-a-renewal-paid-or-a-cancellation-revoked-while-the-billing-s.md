# QA-BILLING-044 — A renewal paid or a cancellation revoked while the billing sweep runs is never overwritten by the sweep

## Preconditions

A Safepay-billed subscription the period-boundary sweep has read as due (ACTIVE past its period end, or PAST_DUE past its grace), and a second actor changing it before the sweep writes.

## Steps

1. The sweep reads the subscription as ACTIVE with an ended period; before it writes, a renewal payment moves `currentPeriodEnd` a period forward.
2. The sweep reads `cancelAtPeriodEnd: true`; before it writes, the tenant resumes renewal.
3. Two different paid checkouts for one invoice are settled at the same moment (DB-backed).
4. A renewal is paid after the subscription lapsed and switched renewals off.

## Expected Result

- The sweep's write is conditional on every field the transition was decided from (status, `currentPeriodEnd`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd`, `gracePeriodEndsAt`), so steps 1 and 2 leave the subscription ACTIVE and untouched.
- Step 3 credits one payment and raises exactly one refund request for the other (`managed-payment-settlement.e2e-spec.ts`).
- Step 4 turns renewals back on (`autoRenew: true`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd: false`).
