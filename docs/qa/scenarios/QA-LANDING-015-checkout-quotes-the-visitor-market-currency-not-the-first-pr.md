# QA-LANDING-015 — Checkout quotes the visitor market currency, not the first price listed

## Preconditions

A published plan carrying prices in at least two currencies whose codes sort
differently from the visitor's market currency — for example QAR and USD, viewed
from a USD market. `/public/plans` returns them ordered by currency ascending, so
QAR is listed first.

## Steps

1. Open `/plans` and note the currency shown.
2. Open `/subscribe` with no query parameters and note the currency.
3. Open `/subscribe?planPriceId=<the QAR price id>` from the same USD market.
4. Open `/` and note the currency on the pricing preview.

## Expected Result

- Steps 1, 2 and 4 all show the market's currency — the same one — and the amount
  shown is the price in that currency, not merely a label over another price.
- Step 3 keeps the plan the link named and re-resolves its price in the market's
  currency. It neither honours the foreign price nor falls back to the first plan
  in the list.
- Where the market has no price for a plan at all, the currency shown is still
  the market's, so the "not published for your region" message names a region
  consistently.

## Notes

A single-currency fixture cannot fail this way, which is why nine existing cases
passed throughout the defect's life. The fixture has to carry two currencies in
the order the API returns them.

Automated at `apps/landing/lib/subscribe-selection.spec.ts`. Steps against the
live site are manual and depend on [[QA-BILLING-015]] having repaired the market.
