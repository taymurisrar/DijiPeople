# QA-ATTENDANCE-011 — Only the requester may withdraw a correction, and not after it is decided

## Preconditions

A pending correction request filed by one employee, with a different employee
assigned as its approver. A second request that has already been decided, and a
third already withdrawn.

## Steps

1. As the requester, withdraw their own pending request.
2. As an unrelated employee, attempt to withdraw it.
3. As the assigned approver, attempt to withdraw it.
4. Attempt to withdraw the already-decided request.
5. Attempt to withdraw the already-withdrawn request.

## Expected Result

- The requester may withdraw their own pending request.
- Anyone else is refused, **including the assigned approver**. Withdrawal is not
  a route around separation of duties, and this is the assertion most likely to
  be lost in a later refactor that treats the approver as generally privileged.
- A decided request cannot be withdrawn, and a withdrawn one cannot be withdrawn
  again.
