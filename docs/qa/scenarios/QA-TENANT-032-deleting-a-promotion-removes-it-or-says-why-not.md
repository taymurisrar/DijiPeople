# QA-TENANT-032 — Deleting a promotion removes it or says why not

## Preconditions

- Platform Admin, signed in as a `PLATFORM_OWNER`.
- One promotion never redeemed, and one with at least one redemption.

## Steps

1. Run `npm --workspace api run test -- promotion-deletion`.
2. Open **Discounts & promotions**.
3. On the never-redeemed promotion, press **Delete** and confirm.
4. On the redeemed promotion, look at the actions column.
5. On an active promotion, press **Deactivate**.

## Expected Result

- The suite passes.
- The confirmation names the promotion being deleted, not "this item".
- The row disappears and stays gone after a reload.
- The redeemed promotion offers no Delete, and says why.
- Deactivate still works and leaves the row in place, now inactive.

## Notes

The old behaviour is the thing to watch for on a retest: `DELETE` returned 200
with the record body and changed nothing but `isActive`. A pass that looks like
"the request succeeded" is not enough — reload and confirm the row is actually
gone.

If a redeemed promotion is somehow deleted through the API, that is a regression
in the rule rather than in the route: the line is redemption, because that is
where commercial history starts.
