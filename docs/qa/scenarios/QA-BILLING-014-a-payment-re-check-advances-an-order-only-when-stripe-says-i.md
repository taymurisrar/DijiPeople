# QA-BILLING-014 — A payment re-check advances an order only when Stripe says it was paid

## Preconditions

A subscription order at `PENDING_PAYMENT` with a Stripe checkout session id, and
a stubbed Stripe client. No database.

## Steps

1. Re-check an order Stripe reports as `unpaid`.
2. Re-check with Stripe unreachable.
3. Re-check an order Stripe reports as `paid`.
4. Inspect the audit entry after an outcome that advanced nothing.
5. Re-check a customer with no recheckable order.
6. Re-check a customer with two orders.

## Expected Result

1. and 2. `confirmPayment` is not called. An unreachable provider is not
   evidence of anything — failing open would let an outage confirm payments.
3. `confirmPayment` is called with the session id. Through the webhook’s own
   path, not a status write: that is what puts `PAYMENT_CONFIRMED` in the outbox
   and gets a tenant provisioned.
4. `BILLING_PAYMENT_RECHECKED` is logged anyway.
5. Refused with `NO_RECHECKABLE_ORDER`.
6. The **newest** is chosen, and `ABANDONED`/`ACTIVATED` are not offered.

## Notes

This path can move an order to `PAID` and start a provisioning run on a button
press, and it had no test of any kind — see [[ITEM-0076]]. The assertions are
aimed at the refusals, because the failure that matters is the platform
witnessing its own payment.

Step 6 is not pedantry: a customer who abandoned one checkout and completed
another has two orders, and re-checking the older one reports "not paid" about
somebody who has paid — confidently wrong, and the operator relays it.
