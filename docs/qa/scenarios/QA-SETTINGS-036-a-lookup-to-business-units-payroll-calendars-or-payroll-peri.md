# QA-SETTINGS-036 — A lookup to business units, payroll calendars or payroll periods lists and searches its records

## Preconditions

A tenant admin (global admin role) in a tenant with at least one business unit,
payroll calendar and payroll period. The API runs with the global
ValidationPipe exactly as `main.ts` configures it (`forbidNonWhitelisted`).

## Steps

1. `GET /business-units?pageSize=50&search=<part of a unit name>`.
2. `GET /payroll/calendars?pageSize=50&search=<part of a calendar name>`.
3. `GET /payroll/periods?pageSize=50&search=<part of a period name>`.
4. `GET /payroll/calendars` with no paging parameters.

## Expected Result

- Steps 1–3 answer 200 with `{ items, meta }`, and `items` holds only records
  whose name matches the search, case-insensitively.
- Step 4 answers 200 with a bare array, as before, so the payroll list pages
  that map over the response keep working.
