# QA-BILLING-031 — Two plan cards with different feature sets never render the same bullets

## Preconditions

A catalog where two adjacent plans share their first several features in
category order and differ only further down the list. On the demo tenant, Growth
and Enterprise are that pair.

## Steps

1. Open `/settings/subscription/plans`.
2. Read the feature bullets on each plan card.
3. Compare the bullets on two plans whose underlying feature sets differ.
4. Follow the counted "and N more" affordance on a card whose list is bounded.

## Expected Result

No two plan cards render identical feature lists while their feature sets
differ. A card that omits features says how many it omitted and links to the full
comparison, so the checkmarks never imply a complete list. Every feature a plan
includes is reachable from its card in one interaction.

## Notes

The original defect was a fixed-height card design meeting a catalog that grew:
a plain cut at eight, applied to a list whose ordering happened to put every
differentiator past the cut. Growth and Enterprise advertised identical
capability while their prices differed by 64%, which gave a buyer no reason to
choose the more expensive one.

Ordering is the load-bearing part here, not the cap. A cap over a
differentiator-first ordering is safe; the same cap over category ordering is
not.
