# QA-CONTRACT-002 — A partner agreement template offers only the placeholder groups valid for its type

## Preconditions

A `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` template being authored; a `CUSTOMER_AGREEMENT` with no
`customerAccountId` and no `relatedLeadId`, and one with a `relatedLeadId`.

## Steps

1. `GET /contracts/placeholder-definitions?contractType=PARTNER_AGREEMENT`.
2. Attempt to save a `PARTNER_AGREEMENT` template whose body includes
   `{{customer.legalName}}`.
3. Call `contractInstanceContextEntities(CUSTOMER_AGREEMENT, {})`.
4. Call the same with `{ relatedLeadId }`.
5. Send a `CUSTOMER_AGREEMENT` with no customer/lead/onboarding linked and a
   required `{{customer.legalName}}` token.

## Expected Result

Step 1 excludes `customer`/`lead`/`tenant`/`implementation` groups. Step 2 is
refused with a `400` naming the out-of-context token. Step 3 excludes
`customer`; step 4 includes it. Step 5 fails with "Customer legal name cannot
be resolved because this agreement is not associated with a customer," not a
generic missing-value error.
