# QA-BILLING-046 — A payment method is public only when its market, currency, approval and live readiness all agree

## Preconditions

A disposable database with Pakistan/PKR and United States/USD markets, the
Starter, Growth and Enterprise plans published in both markets, and payment
method configurations that can vary independently by enabled state, market,
currency, provider environment, merchant approval and credential readiness.
No real credentials or bank account details are used.

## Steps

1. Configure an approved, enabled PKR bank-transfer method with a verified
   receiving account; configure Safepay as sandbox and Stripe as test/unready.
2. Read the Pakistan public commercial configuration and plans, then open
   checkout for each published plan and both billing cycles.
3. Remove the approved receiving account while leaving the method enabled and
   repeat the reads and checkout attempt.
4. In turn, make the bank method disabled, unapproved, wrong-currency and
   wrong-market; repeat after each change.
5. Configure Safepay as enabled but sandbox, then as production without complete
   approved live credentials.
6. Read the United States public configuration and open USD checkout with
   Stripe disabled/test/unconfigured, then with explicitly enabled live-ready
   Stripe.
7. Attempt to select every hidden method by posting its key directly rather
   than choosing it from the rendered list.

## Expected Result

- Pakistan offers bank transfer only while every server-side gate passes. The
  public response contains the customer-facing method and masked instructions,
  never configuration secrets or an unapproved account.
- With no approved receiving account, Pakistan exposes the honest **Request
  Bank Transfer Invoice** assisted-sales fallback. It creates no payable order,
  invoice, payment or tenant and invents no account data.
- Safepay sandbox never appears as a live payment method in a production-like
  environment. Missing approval or live readiness fails closed.
- United States never falls back to a Pakistan method. USD Stripe appears only
  when explicitly enabled and live-ready; otherwise checkout states that online
  payment is unavailable without exposing configuration internals.
- A client-supplied hidden/disabled method is refused by the write path with the
  same coarse unavailable response as an unknown method. Filtering the read is
  not authorization.
- Starter, Growth and Enterprise remain the predefined set. No configuration
  path manufactures another predefined plan or a zero-priced custom plan.
