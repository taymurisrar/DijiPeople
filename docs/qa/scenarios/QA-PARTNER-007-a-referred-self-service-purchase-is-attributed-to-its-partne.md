# QA-PARTNER-007 — A referred self-service purchase is attributed to its partner

## Preconditions

An active partner with an active referral link, and a published public plan
price.

## Steps

1. `npx jest partner-referral-resolver checkout-customer-record` from
   `services/api`.
2. Manual form: open the landing site at `/?ref=<code>`, navigate to Plans →
   Subscribe **without** submitting a lead, and complete checkout.
3. Repeat with an expired or disabled code, and with no code at all.

## Expected Result

Step 1 passes. After step 2 the `CustomerAccount` carries
`originatingPartnerId`, `originatingReferralLinkId` and `referralCodeSnapshot`
together, with `originChannel = PARTNER_REFERRAL`.

In step 3 the expired code leaves `originatingPartnerId` null and
`originChannel = WEBSITE` while **still recording the code** — a lapsed link is
recoverable evidence, a blank is not. With no code, all three are null and the
channel is `WEBSITE`.

A code that names a partner directly must attribute nothing: resolution is the
server's, because attribution decides commission.
