# QA-EMPLOYEE-004 — The reporting hierarchy dialog shows unclipped cards on hover, focus and tap, and its nodes open records

## Preconditions

An employee record whose reporting chain has a manager with two or more direct
reports, and enough people for the tree to be wider than the dialog.

## Steps

1. Open the record → Reporting Hierarchy → View hierarchy.
2. Read the layout.
3. Hover each node in turn.
4. Tab to a node; press Enter.
5. With touch emulation, tap a node once, then again.
6. Hover a node near the bottom and right edges; scroll the tree.
7. Inspect the dialog in the accessibility tree.
8. Repeat steps 1-3 at 400px.

## Expected Result

- Step 1: no detail card is visible; a Close control is visible top-right and
  holds focus.
- Step 2: siblings sit side by side under their manager with connector lines;
  a wide tree scrolls inside the dialog, not the page.
- Step 3: each hovered node shows its card fully, with no text cut off on the
  left; the card moves to the next node hovered.
- Step 4: focus shows the card; Enter opens that employee's record.
- Step 5: the first tap shows the card; the second opens the record.
- Step 6: the card flips or clamps inside the viewport and stays attached while
  scrolling.
- Step 7: the dialog is named "Reporting hierarchy"; no intro paragraph.
- Step 8: the dialog is usable, with horizontal scroll inside it.
