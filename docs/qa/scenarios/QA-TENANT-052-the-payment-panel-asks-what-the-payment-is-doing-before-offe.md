# QA-TENANT-052 — The payment panel asks what the payment is doing before offering to re-check it

## Preconditions

- Platform Admin, signed in as a `PLATFORM_OWNER`.
- One customer whose payment succeeded and whose workspace is provisioned
  (`DijiPeople Demo` on production is one).
- One customer with an order in `PENDING_PAYMENT`.
- One customer with no order at all.

## Steps

1. Run `npm --workspace api run test -- payment-state`.
2. Open the settled customer's record and look at the Payment panel.
3. Open the pending customer's record and look at the Payment panel.
4. Open the customer with no order and look for a Payment panel.
5. `GET /api/super-admin/customers/<id>/payment-state` for each of the three.

## Expected Result

- The suite passes: every `SubscriptionOrderStatus` maps to exactly one state,
  and no unsettled status reports `CONFIRMED`.
- Step 2 shows a confirmed summary — amount, currency, paid date, order number —
  and **no button**. The most prominent control on a settled account is not an
  invitation to question a payment that arrived.
- Step 3 shows the existing panel unchanged, with "Re-check payment with Stripe"
  and the copyable customer message.
- Step 4 shows nothing at all. A customer record is not the place to explain the
  absence of a payment nobody started — which is also why `DRAFT`, `ABANDONED`
  and `CANCELLED` all read as `NONE`.
- Step 5 returns `CONFIRMED`, `AWAITING` and `NONE` respectively.
