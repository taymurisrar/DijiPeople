# QA-EMPLOYEE-006 — The primary work site changes only through the transactional work-site endpoint

## Preconditions

- A throwaway database; an employee whose primary site is Head Office, with an
  active Warehouse assignment.
- An administrator holding `employees.update` and `attendanceDevices.manage`.

## Steps

1. `PATCH /api/employees/{id}` with `locationId` set to Warehouse's id.
2. `PATCH /api/employees/{id}` with `locationId` unchanged plus another field
   change.
3. `POST /api/employees/{id}/work-sites` for Head Office with new validity dates
   and no `isPrimary`.
4. `POST /api/employees/{id}/work-sites/primary` for Warehouse.
5. `POST /api/employees/{id}/work-sites/primary` for a location with no active
   assignment.
6. In the Form Designer for Employees, open the widget palette.

## Expected Result

- Step 1: 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` with a field error on `locationId`; the record
  is unchanged.
- Step 2: 200; the other field saved.
- Step 3: dates saved; Head Office keeps `isPrimary=true`;
  `Employee.locationId` unchanged.
- Step 4: the old primary, the new primary and `Employee.locationId` change
  together.
- Step 5: refused; nothing written and nothing audited.
- Step 6: no Work Site widget is offered.
