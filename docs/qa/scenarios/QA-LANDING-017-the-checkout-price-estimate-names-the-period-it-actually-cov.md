# QA-LANDING-017 — The checkout price estimate names the period it actually covers

## Preconditions

- The landing site is reachable, and the visitor's market resolves to a currency
  that has **both** a monthly and an annual `PER_SEAT` price for one plan.
- At least the annual price must be renderable. It does **not** need to be
  `checkoutReady` — the estimate line renders whether or not the plan can be
  bought, so this scenario is runnable even while [[BUG-0898]] blocks purchase.
- To check the Stripe half (step 5), the annual price must additionally be
  `checkoutReady`.

## Steps

1. Open `/subscribe?plan=<plan>&billingInterval=YEAR&teamSize=<n>` where `<n>` is
   at or above the plan's minimum seats.
2. Read the estimate line beneath the headline price in the "Selected plan" card
   — the sentence of the form `<n> purchased seats · estimated <total> <period>.`
3. Note the period caption `/plans` gives the same price ("per active employee /
   year" or "/ month").
4. Switch the **Billing** select to `Monthly` and read the estimate line again.
5. If the annual price is purchasable, complete the wizard to Stripe Checkout and
   read the amount and period Stripe states.

## Expected Result

- **Step 2/3** — with an annual price selected, the estimate names a *yearly*
  period, and the figure equals `unitAmount × seats` for that year. It must not
  say "per month" while quoting an annual total.
- **Step 4** — with a monthly price selected, the estimate names a monthly
  period and the figure equals `unitAmount × seats` per month.
- **Step 5** — the period and amount Stripe states agree with what the page
  quoted. A page that says "per month" against a Stripe subscription billed
  "per year" fails this scenario even if the *number* matches.
