# QA-CONTRACT-012 — An executed agreement's signed and regenerated copies show every signature from the evidence

## Preconditions

A platform admin session. One partner agreement with only the partner signing,
and one customer agreement where a DijiPeople (PLATFORM) party also signs, both
created from system templates, approved and ready for signature.

## Steps

1. Send the partner agreement; sign it with a **drawn** signature.
2. Send the customer agreement to the platform signer and the customer; sign
   first **typed** (style Script), then **drawn**.
3. Download each agreement's stored `SIGNED_COPY` PDF.
4. From admin, choose **Generate document** on each executed agreement
   (`POST /contracts/:id/generate/pdf`).
5. `POST /contracts/:id/generate/html` on either agreement.

## Expected Result

- Steps 3 and 4 produce copies whose signature section shows, for every
  signer, the drawn mark as an image (or the typed name in its chosen style),
  the signer's name, the method, the verified hash and the real signing time;
  no "Pending", no "Not signed", no `{{`.
- The partner agreement has no DijiPeople signature line; the customer
  agreement has one (ADR-0021).
- Both copies carry the electronic signature appendix and audit certificate;
  only the step-3 copy is the immutable `SIGNED_COPY`.
- Step 5 returns 400 `VALIDATION_INVALID_FORMAT`, not 500.
