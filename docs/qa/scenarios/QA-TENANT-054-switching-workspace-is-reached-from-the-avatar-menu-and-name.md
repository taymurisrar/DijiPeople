# QA-TENANT-054 — Switching workspace is reached from the avatar menu and named once

## Preconditions

- A user account that belongs to **two or more** workspaces, so
  `/workspaces/mine` returns more than one entry. With one workspace the
  switcher renders nothing at all, and the visual half of this scenario has
  nothing to observe — which is the common case and is correct.
- Signed into the tenant product on the workspace hostname.

## Steps

1. Run `npm --workspace web run test -- workspace-switcher-placement`.
2. Sign in and look at the band between the page header and the record action
   bar, on both a list screen and a record screen.
3. Open the avatar menu at the top right.
4. Close it with `Escape`, then reopen it using the keyboard alone — Tab to the
   avatar button, then Enter.
5. With a screen reader running, move through the open menu.

## Expected Result

- Step 1 passes. It asserts the placement invariants directly against the
  source: one `<WorkspaceSwitcher />` in the layout and it is inside a
  `<Suspense>`, the topbar forwards `workspaceSection`, the menu renders it and
  never fetches `/workspaces/mine` itself, the switcher is not a `<details>`,
  and the string "Switch workspace" appears exactly once in it.
- Step 2 shows **nothing** in that band. The row the switcher used to occupy is
  gone.
- Step 3 shows, under the identity card, a separator, the label
  "Switch workspace", and one entry per other workspace with its hostname
  beneath the name. A workspace that cannot be opened is listed but is not a
  link, and says why.
- Step 4 closes and reopens the menu; the switcher is reachable without a mouse.
- Step 5 announces "Switch workspace" **once**, not twice.
- The active workspace is still identifiable without opening the menu — the
  topbar eyebrow and the sidebar's "Active tenant" both name it.

## Notes

ITEM-0102. The doubled announcement in step 5 is the specific defect the record
reported: a visually hidden "Switch workspace." sat beside a visible
"Switch workspace" on the same disclosure.

Step 1 is the durable half and runs in CI. Steps 2–5 need a genuinely
multi-workspace account, which the demo tenant does not have — the unit
assertions exist precisely because the visual check is not reproducible on
demand here. `apps/web` has no jsdom and no testing library, so the automated
half reads the source rather than rendering it; see `apps/web/jest.config.js`.
