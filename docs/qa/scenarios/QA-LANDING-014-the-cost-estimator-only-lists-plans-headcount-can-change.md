# QA-LANDING-014 — The cost estimator only lists plans headcount can change

## Preconditions

A region with at least one flat-priced plan and, ideally, one per-seat plan.

## Steps

1. `npm --workspace landing run test -- plan-estimator`
2. Open `/plans` and scroll to **Estimate your cost**.
3. Change Active employees between 25, 50 and 100.
4. Check which plans are listed.
5. If the region has no per-seat plan at all, read what the section says.

## Expected Result

Step 3: **every** figure shown changes.

Step 4: only per-seat plans appear. No flat-priced plan, and no plan showing "On
request" beside real prices.

Step 5: the section explains that every plan is a flat price and points at the
cards above. It renders no headcount control.

## Notes

Step 3 is the whole scenario. A figure that does not move under a control that
claims to move it teaches the visitor the control does nothing — so a genuine
per-seat estimate later is not believed either.

Consideration: the minimum-seat case is worth exercising by hand if a plan has
one. Entering fewer employees than the minimum must show the minimum's price
*and say so* — a page quoting six seats while Stripe charges ten is BUG-0080's
shape, and it is the reason `belowMinimum` exists rather than a silent
`Math.max`.
