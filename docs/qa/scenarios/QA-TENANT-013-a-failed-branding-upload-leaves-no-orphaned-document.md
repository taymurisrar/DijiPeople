# QA-TENANT-013 — A failed branding upload leaves no orphaned document

## Preconditions

A tenant with `branding.manage`, and a PNG under 3 MB.

## Steps

1. `npx jest branding-assets.service.spec` from `services/api`.
2. Manual form: Settings → Branding, upload a logo.
3. Upload a PDF, and a PNG over 3 MB.

## Expected Result

Step 1 passes. Step 2 stores the document, points `logoUrl` at it and records
`logoDocumentId` alongside. Step 3 is refused **by the API**, not only by the
browser — the same request sent directly to `POST
/tenant-settings/branding-assets` is refused identically.

And the property that matters most: when the settings write fails after the
upload succeeds, the document created by the first step is archived. No
orphan is left that nothing references and nothing can find.
