# QA-BILLING-013 — Exactly one stored flag decides whether a plan can be bought

## Preconditions

None. This reads the three source files that held a purchasability gate.

## Steps

1. Scan `billing.service.ts`, `commercial-config.service.ts` and
   `super-admin.service.ts` for any read of or filter on `Plan.isPublic`,
   ignoring comments.
2. Confirm the API response still exposes an `isPublic` field, and that it is
   computed from `publicationStatus` rather than stored.
3. Confirm every remaining gate compares against `PUBLISHED`.

## Expected Result

Step 1 finds nothing. Steps 2 and 3 hold.

## Notes

[[BUG-0223]]: `Plan` carried two independent answers to *may a customer buy
this?*. `isPublic` was a boolean defaulting to **true**, with no audit columns
and no operator write path at all — changeable only by a seed or by hand in the
database. `publicationStatus` is DRAFT/PUBLISHED/ARCHIVED with `publishedAt` and
`archivedAt`, applies to plan, market and price alike, and was already the
authority in `commercial-offer.resolver.ts`.

Two gates that can disagree is worse than either alone. A plan could be
PUBLISHED and `isPublic: false` — visible in the offer, refused at checkout — or
DRAFT and purchasable. Nothing detected either, because each gate was correct on
its own terms.

**This reads source deliberately.** The defect is the *existence* of a second
gate, not the behaviour of any one call, and a behavioural test would have to
guess which of the eleven read sites somebody might reintroduce. Step 2 is the
paired assertion: removing the reads without keeping the derived response field
would be a silent contract break, since the landing site consumes it.

Scoped to `Plan.isPublic` only. The route decorator `IS_PUBLIC_KEY` and
`isPublicSafeReason` share a prefix and are unrelated.
