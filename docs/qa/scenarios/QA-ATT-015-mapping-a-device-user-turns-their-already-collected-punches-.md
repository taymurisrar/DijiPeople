# QA-ATT-015 — Mapping a device user turns their already-collected punches into attendance

## Preconditions

- An attendance integration that is ACTIVE, with a paired gateway and a verified
  device.
- At least one completed sync, so raw punches exist for a device user who has
  **not** been mapped to an employee. The order matters: this scenario is about
  punches that arrived before anyone said who they belonged to, which is the
  normal order of operations.
- An employee to map that device user to.

## Steps

1. Open the mapping workspace and confirm the device user shows as `UNMATCHED`.
2. Map it to the employee (`POST /integrations/attendance/external-users/{id}/map`).
   Note the `backfilledEvents` count in the response.
3. Wait one reconciliation cycle. The queue drains every 30 seconds.
4. Read the employee's attendance day for the date those punches fall on
   (`GET /attendance/engine/days/{employeeId}/{date}`).
5. Separately, map a device user who has **no** punches yet and confirm nothing
   is queued for them.

## Expected Result

1. The mapping succeeds and reports the number of punches it attributed.
2. Within a cycle, and with **no manual reconciliation**, the attendance day
   exists: a session whose `startSource` is `DEVICE`, at the device's work site,
   with `firstCheckInAt` derived from the device's own timezone rather than the
   server's.
3. Mapping a user with no punches queues nothing — there is no day to build.
4. A failure to queue does not fail the mapping. The mapping is what the operator
   asked for and is already durable; the day stays reachable through Recalculate.

## Notes

Created 2026-09-09, guarding [[BUG-2933]] — the mapping service never called
`requeueForMapping`, so backfilled punches were attributed correctly and never
turned into attendance.

`AUTOMATION_STATUS` is **PARTIAL**, and the split is worth stating. The automated
test covers the call, its absence when nothing was backfilled, and the failure
path. What it cannot cover is the part that made this bug invisible: every status
indicator was green while the outcome was missing. Step 4 is the assertion that
matters and it spans the queue, a background drain and the reconciliation engine.

Re-running by hand takes about two minutes against any tenant with a working
integration. **Do not stop at "events ingested"** — that is precisely where the
original validation would have stopped and declared success.
