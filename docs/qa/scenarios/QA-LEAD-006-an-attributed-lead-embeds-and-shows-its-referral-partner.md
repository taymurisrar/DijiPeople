# QA-LEAD-006 — An attributed lead embeds and shows its referral partner

## Preconditions

A lead attributed to a partner (automatically via referral code, or manually
via attribution correction).

## Steps

1. Call `getLead` for the attributed lead.
2. Open the lead's record page in `apps/admin`.
3. Open the partner-attribution panel on the same page.

## Expected Result

Step 1's response embeds `partner { id, displayName, type, status }` and
nothing more about the partner. Step 2's lookup field shows the partner's
name, not a blank label. Step 3's panel shows "Current partner" with the
attributed partner's name/type/status.
