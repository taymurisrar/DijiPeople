# QA-ADMINUX-001 — Sticky console chrome and the agreement Fields & Signatures panel stay in view on a long document

## Preconditions

Platform Admin signed in as a platform super admin; a contract template whose document is taller than the viewport.

## Steps

1. Open the template at 1366x768.
2. Scroll the document to the middle, then to the end.
3. Repeat at 1280x720.

## Expected Result

The command bar and editor toolbar stay pinned; the Fields & Signatures panel stays directly below the toolbar, scrolls on its own, and shrinks rather than sliding under the toolbar at the end of the document. The unit check fails if html or body regain `overflow-x: hidden`.
