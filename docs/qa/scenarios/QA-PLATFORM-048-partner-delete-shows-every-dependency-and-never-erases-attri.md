# QA-PLATFORM-048 — Partner delete shows every dependency and never erases attribution

## Preconditions

A platform admin, plus a partner that has a referral link and an attributed customer.

## Steps

1. In services/api, run `npx jest src/modules/partners`. In apps/admin, run `npx jest lib/runtime/dependency-delete-model.spec.ts`.
2. Browser: on the partner record, click Delete.
3. Follow the dependency links, remove the blockers, then delete again from the list.

## Expected Result

- The dialog lists the referral link (blocks) and the attributed customer (retain), each with a link.
- Confirm is disabled.
- After the blockers are removed, the delete succeeds, the timeline is removed with the partner, and the deletion is audited.
