# QA-RUNTIME-039 — The approvals inbox shows a full record and decides through the owning module

## Preconditions

- A tenant with at least one **pending** leave request and one **pending**
  attendance correction, each with an approval step assigned to a user you can
  sign in as. The demo tenant satisfies this.
- Three accounts, or one account whose role can be varied between runs:
  1. **the assigned approver**, holding `leave-requests.approve` /
     `leave-requests.reject` and the matching matrix privileges;
  2. **a reader**, holding an approvals read key but none of the owning module's
     approve or reject keys;
  3. **the requester** who raised one of the pending requests.
- Note before starting: `hasElevatedTenantRole` covers `global-admin` and
  `system-admin` and **bypasses the permission check entirely**. Running step 5
  as either of those proves nothing. Use a role that is neither.

## Steps

1. Open **Approvals**. Note the Approval, Module, Requester and Submitted At
   shown on a pending row.
2. Click that row.
3. On the record page, read the same four fields, plus **Assigned To**.
4. As the **assigned approver**, use **Approve**, entering an optional comment.
   Then open the underlying leave request or attendance correction directly.
5. As the **reader**, POST to the approve route for a pending request they are
   assigned to.
6. As the **requester**, open a request they raised and look at **Withdraw**.
7. Open an approval whose module has no delegate — payroll, timesheet, benefit,
   claim or loan — and read the command bar.
8. Open an approval that is already Approved or Rejected and read the command bar.
9. Request the approvals list with an invalid status filter.

## Expected Result

1–3. The record page shows the **same** values as the list row — no blank
fields, and no "Unknown requester" for a request whose submitter exists.
Assigned To names the pending step's assignees. Below the form, the approval
chain lists every step in order with its status, the history lists each action
with its comment and author, and a link opens the source record.

4. The command succeeds. **The underlying record is decided too** — the leave
request reads APPROVED with its balance consumed, or the attendance correction
reads approved and its entry amended. This is the assertion that matters: a
version that advanced only the approval row would leave the leave request
PENDING while this screen said APPROVED.

5. **403.** Being able to read the inbox, and being the assigned approver, is not
permission to approve. The refusal must match what the leave module's own
approve route would give the same caller.

6. **Withdraw** is offered to the requester and not to an approver who is not
also the requester. Withdrawing cancels the underlying request.

7. Approve and Reject are disabled, and the reason names the module and where the
decision is made — "Payroll approvals are decided on the Payroll record…" — not
a caption about adapter handlers. The source-record link goes to that module.

8. Approve and Reject are disabled and the reason is "This request is already
approved." (or rejected, or cancelled), distinct from step 7's reason.

9. **400**, not 500. The status is validated rather than cast straight to a
Prisma enum.

## Notes

Created 2026-08-31 at `2b001494`.

Steps 1–3, 5, 7, 8 and the client wiring for 4 are automated in
`services/api/src/modules/approvals/approvals.decision.spec.ts` and
`apps/web/lib/runtime/modules/approval-decision-commands.spec.ts`.

**Step 4's second half is the manual part, and it is the point of this
scenario.** The automated tests assert that the delegate is *called*; only a live
run confirms the leave request itself moved and the balance was consumed.
Automating that needs a seeded tenant with a resolvable approval chain, which
[[BUG-1968-leave-approval-routing-requires-an-active-reporting-manager-]]
records as hard to arrange on a fresh tenant — every matched rule in the chain
must resolve, and a freshly provisioned tenant has neither an activated
reporting manager nor an active HR user.
