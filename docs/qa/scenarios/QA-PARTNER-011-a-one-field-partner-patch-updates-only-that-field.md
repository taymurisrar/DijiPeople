# QA-PARTNER-011 — A one-field partner PATCH updates only that field

## Preconditions

An existing COMPANY partner with a `companyName`.

## Steps

1. `PATCH /partners/:id` with `{ "notes": "..." }` alone.
2. `PATCH /partners/:id` with `{ "taxId": "..." }` alone.
3. `PATCH /partners/:id` with an empty body.
4. `PATCH /partners/:id` with an undeclared or invalid field.
5. `PATCH /partners/:id` clearing the COMPANY partner's `companyName`.
6. `PATCH /partners/:id` switching `type` to INDIVIDUAL without supplying a
   contact name.

## Expected Result

Steps 1-3 succeed, each writing only the field(s) actually sent — `type`,
`displayName`, `email` and `status` are untouched and `status` is never reset
to `DRAFT`. Step 4 is still rejected. Steps 5-6 are refused — the type-policy
check runs against the record as it would read after the patch.
