# QA-PLATFORM-024 — Every admin route and sidebar item is reachable and renders its own screen

## Preconditions

A platform user signed in to the admin console. No seeded data is required — this
scenario asserts reachability, not content.

## Steps

1. Sign in and capture the sidebar: every internal anchor inside the shell's
   navigation.
2. Enumerate every declared route — the `page.tsx` files under
   `apps/admin/app/(internal)`, excluding dynamic segments.
3. Visit the union of the two. For each, record the HTTP status, the heading the
   page renders, any page error, and any response of 400 or worse.

## Expected Result

Every route responds **200** and renders its own heading — not only the shell's.
Every sidebar item resolves to one of those routes.

**No screen fires a 4xx while loading.** A screen that renders correctly while
requesting routes that do not exist is the shape of BUG-1419: 25 incident links
on `/settings/monitoring` all returned 404, Next prefetched them, and the only
outward symptom was that the page took six times longer than its siblings.
A reachability check alone would have passed it.

Load time is worth recording per route even though this scenario does not gate
on it — an outlier is usually a defect rather than a slow screen.

## Notes

Established 2026-08-26 against production at `8d6be21b`: 63 routes, all 200; 19
sidebar items, all resolving. That same pass found BUG-1419 through the 4xx
check, not the status check.

Re-run after any change to the shell, the sidebar, or the platform module
registry — the registry is what builds record routes, and a `routeBase` with no
page behind it is exactly what BUG-1419 was.
