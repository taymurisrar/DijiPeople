# QA-SETTINGS-032 — Delivery Logs show a reason column, an In-app channel and one name

## Preconditions

- A tenant with email delivery log rows in SENT, FAILED and NOT_DELIVERED, and
  in-app notifications sent to several users.
- An administrator holding `notification.logs.read`; a second user without it.

## Steps

1. Open Settings → Notifications; open each card.
2. Open Delivery Logs.
3. Click an email row.
4. Choose Channel → In-app; page forward; choose Email.
5. As the user without `notification.logs.read`, call
   `GET /api/notifications/in-app-delivery-logs`.
6. View Delivery Logs at 400px.

## Expected Result

- Step 1: cards read "Notification Rules", "Email Templates", "Email Providers"
  and "Delivery Logs"; each opened page title matches its card.
- Step 2: NOT_DELIVERED and FAILED rows show text in the Reason column; no row
  checkboxes.
- Step 3: the record opens, with Retry when permitted.
- Step 4: the URL gains `?channel=in-app`; rows list the notification,
  recipient, status, read and created times; rows do not navigate; paging keeps
  the channel; Email returns to the email log.
- Step 5: 403.
- Step 6: the channel switch and table fit; the table scrolls inside itself.
