# QA-PLATFORM-052 — Partner commissions follow their ledger lifecycle and agreements keep their snapshot

## Preconditions

A platform admin, plus a partner with a default commission of 10 and currency USD.

## Steps

1. In services/api, run the partner commission lifecycle and service specs and `contracts.commission-snapshot.spec.ts`.
2. Browser: create an agreement from the partner and check that commission (10%) and currency (USD) are pre-filled.
3. Browser: change the partner's default commission to 15 and confirm the agreement still shows 10%.
4. Browser: add a commission and move it through Approve, Payable and Paid. Try to void it after it is paid.

## Expected Result

- All specs pass.
- The agreement keeps its snapshot after the partner default changes.
- The commission amount is calculated on the server.
- Illegal transitions are refused.
- A paid commission is final.
