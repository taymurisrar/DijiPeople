# QA-TENANT-015 — Erasing one tenant leaves every other tenant complete

## Preconditions

Two fixture tenants against a real PostgreSQL, seeded with the **same shape** of
data: the payroll chain that produced [[BUG-0148]], and the full commercial chain
erasure deliberately keeps — subscription, invoice, contract, support case,
onboarding, order, refund, and the `SupportCaseIncident` joining a retained
support case to a tenant error log.

Seeding both identically is the point. A neighbour holding nothing would let a
wrong predicate pass because there was nothing of that kind to delete.

## Steps

1. Snapshot the neighbour across every question the erasure plan makes it
   possible to get wrong — built from the three constants, not hand-listed:
   - a row count per model in `TENANT_ERASURE_DELETE_ORDER` (242 of them);
   - for each `TENANT_ERASURE_DETACHED_MODELS` entry, the model **and each
     cleared field**, counted as "still populated";
   - for each `TENANT_ERASURE_LINK_CLEANUPS` entry, the relation-scoped row.
2. Confirm every group is actually populated. A group at zero compares zero to
   zero afterwards and asserts nothing.
3. Erase the other tenant through the production sequence — the same detach,
   link-cleanup, self-reference and delete-order walk `TenantErasureService`
   runs.
4. Re-take the snapshot and diff it.
5. Confirm the neighbour's own `Tenant` row is present **and usable** — a row
   whose contents were cascaded away still satisfies `findUnique`.
6. Confirm the probe count equals the plan size, so an empty collection cannot
   make step 4 vacuously true.

## Expected Result

Step 4 reports no differences. Steps 2, 5 and 6 hold.

## Notes

For an irreversible cross-tenant operation the assertion that matters is not
"the tenant is gone" — that is the easy half — but "the other tenant is
untouched". Erasure is 242 `deleteMany` calls whose correctness rests entirely on
a `tenantId` predicate; one missing predicate deletes a neighbour's rows, the
transaction commits, and nothing notices unless a neighbour is there to notice
with. [[QA-TENANT-005]] covers the delete **order**, on a single tenant.

**Detachment is not deletion**, which is why the detached models are probed per
cleared field. Erasure nulls `Contract.subscriptionId` and its siblings twice —
by `tenantId`, then along the relation `{ subscription: { tenantId } }`. The
second clear is the one that can reach a neighbour's row while leaving the row
present, so a count would report "still there" over a record quietly gutted.
These are contracts, orders, refunds and support history belonging to a
different paying customer.

Driven from the three constants rather than a hand-written list, so a model added
to erasure later is covered the moment it is added. Registered as REG-220 and
raised as [[ITEM-0003]].
