## Migration findings (legacy `enterprise-plus`)

Evidence: `evidence/RUN007-migration-fixtures.md` (before/after tables, ids, plan prices) and `results-legacy-{a,b,c}.json`.

- **Identity:** the legacy plan row keeps its **id** and is renamed in place to `custom-<first 12 hex of id>` / `Custom Plan <ID6>`, `kind = CUSTOM`, `salesModel = CUSTOM_ONLY`, `isPublic = false`; the old key and name survive only in `metadataJson.convertedFromLegacyPlan`. **No plan, in any state, carries `enterprise-plus` in its key or name** (MIG-001/005, CAT-002); the catalogue is exactly Starter, Growth, Enterprise, all `CATALOGUE`.
- **Reservation:** fixtures A and B (several customers) → not reserved and **ARCHIVED**; fixture C (exactly one customer) → **reserved to that customer**, publication unchanged. In both cases existing subscribers keep their subscription.
- **Subscriptions, terms and history:** subscription ids, status, cycle, base/final price, currency, validity dates, plan-price link, invoices and payments are identical before and after (MIG-003/006/007); **no table holds a plan or plan-price reference that points nowhere** (information_schema sweep over every `planId` / `selectedPlanId` / `agreedPlanId` column).
- **Entitlements:** every legacy feature still resolves at runtime for each converted tenant, including the Desktop Agent and white-label e-mail that tenants held without an explicit grant, and the explicit CUSTOM grant (MIG-002/008). This matches owner decision 5 (legacy customers keep contracted terms and entitlements until a separate commercial review); no feature was removed or newly granted except the universal basic import/export baseline.
- **Withdrawn plan:** with the converted plan ARCHIVED (A, B) its subscribers stay ACTIVE and keep their entitlements (MIG-008; owner decision 8).
- **Repeatability:** a further `seed:config` changes no plan, feature, price or subscription row (MIG-004; SEEDPLAN-001/002 for dynamically created Custom plans, including one named "Starter").
- **Not satisfied:** the converted plan stays assignable by an operator to any other customer's tenant ([[DP-QA-043]]); it is also still in the staff-facing operator list (active and priced). Customer-facing paths refuse it.
