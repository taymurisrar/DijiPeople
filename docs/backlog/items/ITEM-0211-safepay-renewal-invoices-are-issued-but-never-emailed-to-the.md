# ITEM-0211 — Safepay renewal invoices are issued but never emailed to the tenant

## Summary

`ManagedRenewalService` issues a renewal invoice `MANAGED_BILLING_RENEWAL_NOTICE_DAYS` before the period end, but nothing emails it. The tenant sees it in billing history and the overview warns once it is overdue.

## Why It Matters

A tenant who does not open billing settings lapses into the grace period, and then loses entitlement, without being told a payment was due.

## Evidence

`issueRenewalInvoice` in `services/api/src/modules/billing/services/managed-renewal.service.ts`.

## Proposed Approach

Emit a domain event on issuance and send through the `notifications` module (never directly from billing), with a pay link to `/settings/subscription/billing-history`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Issuing a renewal invoice sends one email to the billing contact.
- A re-run of the sweep does not send a second one.

## Dependencies

None.
