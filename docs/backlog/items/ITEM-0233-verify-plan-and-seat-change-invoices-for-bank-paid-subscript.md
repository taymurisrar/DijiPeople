# ITEM-0233 — Verify plan and seat change invoices for bank-paid subscriptions end to end

## Summary

Plan changes and seat changes for a bank-paid subscription generate invoices through the existing `PlanChangeService`/`SeatChangeService`. Renewals, expiry, refund and recording are proven against a real PostgreSQL (`bank-transfer-settlement.e2e-spec.ts`); proration/upgrade invoices for a `BANK_TRANSFER` subscription are not.

## Why It Matters

If an upgrade invoice for a bank-paid tenant tries to open a gateway checkout, or is never reminded, the tenant is billed but cannot pay it, or a mid-period change is silently free.

## Evidence

`services/api/src/modules/billing/services/plan-change.service.ts`, `seat-change.service.ts`, `managed-checkout.service.ts` (`startInvoicePayment` refuses non-gateway providers); no bank-transfer case in `plan-change.service.spec.ts`.

## Proposed Approach

Add DB-backed cases: upgrade and seat increase on a BANK_TRANSFER subscription produce an open invoice with a `bankReference`, `payable` is false, finance can record it with `recordInvoicePayment`, and the change applies exactly once. Fix whatever the cases expose.

## Acceptance Criteria

Tests pass for upgrade, downgrade at period end and seat increase on a bank-paid subscription, each ending with one paid invoice and the entitlement change applied once.

## Dependencies

The bank-transfer settlement work in PR #136 (EXECPLAN-0056).
