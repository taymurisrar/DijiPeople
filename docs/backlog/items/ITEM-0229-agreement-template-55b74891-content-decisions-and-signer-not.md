# ITEM-0229 — Agreement template 55b74891 content decisions and signer notification gaps

## Summary

The BUG-4133 hotfix made template 55b74891 render correctly and added content
checks, but deliberately did not edit the template's legal text or production
data. What is left needs the owner: the template's own wording, its lifecycle
gate, and two notification and change-request behaviours that were not built
in the hotfix.

## Why It Matters

Until the template is revised, no agreement drafted from it can be sent for
signature: the send gate refuses its 17 distinct "[REQUIRED — …]" /
"[Automatically populate …]" notes and its "Status: Draft — Subject to
Completion and Legal Review" line. Its stored gate `customer_onboarding` is
unsupported and must be replaced on the next save.

## Evidence

Production, read-only, 2026-10-12, published version 2:

- Gate `customer_onboarding`; the supported choice for a master services
  agreement is "Lead to customer conversion" (`LEAD_TO_CUSTOMER`).
- Hardcoded "Agreement Version: 1.0 — Pakistan Pilot" where `{{contract.version}}` now exists.
- Hardcoded liability floor "PKR 100,000", Pakistani governing law, Karachi
  courts, 15-day cure period, 3-year confidentiality survival, 30-day force
  majeure — `{{contract.liabilityCap}}`, `{{contract.governingLaw}}`,
  `{{contract.jurisdiction}}`, `{{contract.curePeriodDays}}` exist for the
  variable ones.
- "Plan Features: [Automatically populate …]" — `{{commercial.planFeatures}}` now exists.
- Support/SLA and data notes (A5–A9, B7–B12, C6) map onto existing
  `sla.*` and `contract.dataRetentionDays` / `contract.dataExportPeriodDays`
  placeholders where the platform holds the value.
- Signer roles are legacy free text, so agreements keep counterparty-only
  signing until the template is saved with structured roles.
- The admin Vercel project did not deploy merge `dcd2720` automatically; it was
  deployed through a Vercel Git-source deployment of `main`.

Not built in the hotfix: signer notification when a request is cancelled or
expires, automatic signing reminders, and an explicit accept/reject decision
record on a change request (today a change request returns the agreement to
draft and the revised version is re-issued, which invalidates every earlier
link).

## Proposed Approach

1. Owner revises template 55b74891 as a new draft: choose the gate, choose the
   signer roles, replace notes with agreed terms or placeholders, then publish.
2. Decide whether cancellation/expiry notices and scheduled reminders should go
   to signers, then build them on the existing platform-communications path.
3. Decide whether change requests need their own accept/reject record.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Template 55b74891's published version passes the content check with no
  "Blocks sending" findings and has a supported gate and structured signer roles.
- Each of the two notification and change-request decisions is recorded as an
  answer, or as a follow-up item if the answer is "build it".
