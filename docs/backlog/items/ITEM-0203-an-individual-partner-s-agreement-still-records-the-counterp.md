# ITEM-0203 — An individual partner's agreement still records the counterparty as a PARTNER party, not INDIVIDUAL

## Summary

`services/api/src/modules/partners/partner-type-policy.ts` declares, per
partner type, the `ContractPartyType` an agreement should record — `PARTNER`
for a COMPANY, `INDIVIDUAL` for an INDIVIDUAL. Nothing reads it: the contracts
module's default-party inference still records every partner counterparty as
`PARTNER`.

## Why It Matters

An individual's agreement describes them as a partner organisation. It is a
labelling fault today (no rule branches on the party type for signing), but
the declaration and the behaviour disagree, and the next reader of the policy
will assume it is enforced.

## Evidence

- TASK-0032 WP-04 report, RISK_AREAS #3 and UNRESOLVED
  (`docs/tasks/TASK-0032-streams/t0032-wp-04-report.md`).
- `contractPartyType` appears only in `partner-type-policy.ts` on
  `develop` at `5496a244`.

## Proposed Approach

Have the contracts module's default-party creation read the policy for a
partner-sourced agreement. Check every consumer of `partyType === 'PARTNER'`
(signing slots, `renderSignatureEvidenceTokens`, the partner portal's contract
list) before switching, since some may mean "any counterparty".

## Acceptance Criteria

- A new agreement from an INDIVIDUAL partner records its counterparty party as
  `INDIVIDUAL`; a COMPANY partner's still records `PARTNER`.
- Signing, evidence rendering and the partner portal behave the same for both.
