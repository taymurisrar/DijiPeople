# ITEM-0208 — Production Neon database exceeded its data transfer quota and failed two deploys

## Summary

The production database (Neon) refused queries with "Your project has exceeded
the data transfer quota. Upgrade your plan to increase limits." while the API
was running. Render's two most recent redeploys of `e253306a` — 2026-09-19
and 2026-09-22 — both ended `pre_deploy_failed` because the pre-deploy
`release` script (migrate, seed:config and the other seeds) could not reach
the database. Production stayed on the 2026-09-13 deploy throughout.

## Why It Matters

While the quota is exhausted every database-backed request fails — sign-in,
the tenant app, the public legal and pricing pages — and no deploy can
succeed. The pre-deploy script itself moves a lot of data (six seed steps on
every deploy), so deploys consume the quota they depend on. Nothing alerted:
the failure was found by reading deploy history during the TASK-0032 release.

## Evidence

- Render deploys for `srv-d7js7fqqqhas739v4i7g`: `pre_deploy_failed` on
  2026-09-19T19:57Z and 2026-09-22T09:06Z (commit `e253306a`).
- Service logs, 2026-09-22 09:06 UTC: `DriverAdapterError: Your project has
  exceeded the data transfer quota` on `GET /api/public/legal` and
  `GET /api/public/commercial-config` (500 `SYSTEM_UNEXPECTED_ERROR`).
- 2026-09-26: the same endpoints answer 200 and the TASK-0032 release
  (`b586ac0a`) deployed with its pre-deploy step passing — the quota had
  reset or been raised in between.

## Proposed Approach

1. Owner: check the Neon plan and usage dashboard; decide on a plan with
   headroom or a spend cap with an alert.
2. Alert on the quota before it is reached, and on `pre_deploy_failed`.
3. Measure the data transfer of the pre-deploy `release` script and trim
   seeds that re-read or rewrite unchanged rows on every deploy.

Needs an ExecPlan only for step 3.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A quota-exhaustion or failed pre-deploy raises an alert someone sees.
- The pre-deploy script's data transfer per deploy is known and bounded.

## Dependencies

Owner access to the Neon account.
