# ITEM-0206 — Admin dashboard drill-downs for active users, failed sign-ins and unresolved errors land on approximate pages

## Summary

The redesigned admin dashboard (ITEM-0199) links every tile to a real page, but
three land on the nearest page rather than a filtered view: "Active users" and
"Failed sign-ins" (no cross-tenant user or sign-in list exists) and
"Unresolved errors" (the error-log page filters one status at a time, not
"everything except resolved").

## Why It Matters

An operator following a spike has to re-filter by hand on the page they land
on, which is the step a drill-down exists to remove.

## Evidence

TASK-0032 WP-07 report, UNRESOLVED
(`docs/tasks/TASK-0032-streams/t0032-wp-07-report.md`).

## Proposed Approach

Add a multi-status (or "open") filter to the platform error-log list and link
"Unresolved errors" to it. A cross-tenant sign-in view needs its own
permission and runtime-module decision; plan it separately.

## Acceptance Criteria

- "Unresolved errors" opens the error log filtered to exactly the unresolved
  statuses, matching the tile's count.
- "Active users" and "Failed sign-ins" either open a matching filtered view or
  are labelled as summary-only.

## Dependencies

None.

## Related Items

[[ITEM-0199]], [[TASK-0032]], [[platform-admin]]

## Resolution

Fixed on `agent/backlog-0203-0204-0206`. `openIncidentWhere()` backs the
monitoring overview count, the operations dashboard's "Errors needing
attention" and a new `open` view; the tile and its attention-list entry link
to `?viewId=open`. The error-log page now passes every named view as its view
key, so "Under investigation" also lists FIX_IN_PROGRESS as its tile counts.
"Active users" and "Failed sign-ins" are no longer links — no screen lists
what they count. REG-632, QA-PLATFORM-043.
