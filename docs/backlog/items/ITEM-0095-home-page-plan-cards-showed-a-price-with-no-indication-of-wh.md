# ITEM-0095 — Home page plan cards showed a price with no indication of what it buys

## Summary

`PlanPreview` on the home page rendered a plan name, a highlight badge, a
description, a price and a button. `/plans` rendered all of that **plus** the
incremental capability list — "Everything in Starter, plus…" with a check mark
per capability, and a feature-catalogue legend below the grid.

So a visitor comparing plans on the front door could see that Growth cost more
than Starter and nothing about what the difference bought. The only way to find
out was to leave the page.

Reported by the owner: *"make sure home and plans page has almost same plan
section. Home page is missing features details."*

## Why It Matters

A pricing preview whose cards cannot answer "what do I get" is doing the one
thing a pricing preview must not do. The number is the least useful half of the
comparison when three tiers differ by capability rather than by volume.

It is also a **drift** finding rather than a missing feature: both surfaces read
the same `CommercialConfigView`, and `incrementalFeatures` / `plansAreCumulative`
were already exported and already used by `/plans`. The data and the helper were
both there. Only the rendering was missing, and nothing asserted the two
surfaces agreed.

## Evidence

- `apps/landing/app/_components/plan-preview.tsx` — rendered `highlightLabel`
  only; no reference to `incrementalFeatures`, `featureCatalog` or `CheckIcon`.
- `apps/landing/app/plans/plans-experience.tsx` — `incrementalFeatures(plans,
  index, featureCatalog)`, a check-marked list, and a catalogue legend.
- Both take `CommercialConfigView`, which already carries `featureCatalog`.

## Proposed Approach

Render the same incremental list on the home preview, from the same helpers,
using the same wording — "Includes" for the first tier, "Everything in X, plus"
after it.

**Deliberately not copied:** the billing-period toggle, the headcount estimator
and the catalogue legend. The component's own header comment argues these belong
to `/plans`, and that argument still holds: each is a *decision* that would then
have two implementations. A feature list is a *fact* with one derivation, which
is why it is safe to render twice.

The parity that matters is derivation, not markup. The two pages are different
layouts on purpose — a preview and a full comparison — and making the JSX
identical would forbid the difference they are supposed to have.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The home preview lists incremental capabilities per plan, with the same
  phrasing as `/plans`.
- Both surfaces derive that list from `incrementalFeatures` and
  `plansAreCumulative` rather than from `plan.features` directly.
- No billing toggle or estimator is added to the home page.
- Landing suite green.

## Resolution

Done on `agent/record-state-reconciliation`. `PlanPreview` now computes
`plansAreCumulative` once for the grid — it is a property of the ladder, not of
a card — and renders `incrementalFeatures` per plan with `CheckIcon`, matching
`/plans` in wording and in derivation.

The feature block also replaced a bare `<div className="mt-5 flex-1" />` spacer
that existed only to push the CTA down; `flex-1` on the list does the same job
and gives the space something to hold.

Guarded by `apps/landing/lib/plan-section-parity.spec.ts`, which asserts both
surfaces use the same five helpers and that neither hand-rolls a list from
`plan.features` — the shortcut a future edit is most likely to take, and one
that would render raw catalogue keys and repeat every inherited capability on
every card.

**Asserted from source rather than by rendering**, because `apps/landing` has no
jsdom. That is a scope decision recorded on [[TASK-0008]] WP-11, not an
oversight, and it bounds what this guard proves: that the derivation is shared,
not that the markup is correct. Removing the derivation fails the suite.

Landing: 11 suites, 149 tests, 0 failures. Typecheck and lint clean.
