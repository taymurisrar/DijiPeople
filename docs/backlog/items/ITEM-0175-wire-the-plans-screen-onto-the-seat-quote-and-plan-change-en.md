# ITEM-0175 — Wire the plans screen onto the seat-quote and plan-change endpoints that now exist

## Summary

The tenant plans screen and the billing API were fixed in the same task, in
parallel, by two streams on branches that could not reference each other. The
server grew exactly the endpoints the screen needed; the screen, unable to call
something that did not yet exist, solved both problems another way. Both halves
are correct and shipped. This item closes the gap between them.

Two specific pieces of wiring. The order summary on a plan card recomputes the
seat-pricing rule in the browser, and a seat-quote endpoint now returns it. A
tenant with a live subscription is told to change plans in the payment portal,
and a plan-change endpoint with a real proration quote now exists.


## Why It Matters

Neither gap is a defect today, which is why this is an item and not a bug. The
cost is duplication and a worse experience, both of which get more expensive the
longer they sit.

The seat arithmetic now has two implementations. That exact rule already had to
be de-duplicated once on the server after two copies disagreed, and this is the
third copy. Both are pinned to the same fixtures so they cannot drift silently
right now, but that guard is a test somebody could delete, not a structural
guarantee.

The plan change is a worse experience than the product can already deliver.
Sending a customer to an external portal to change a plan means leaving the
product, losing the comparison they were reading, and coming back to a screen
that may not reflect what they just did. The endpoint quotes the proration
before confirmation, which the portal does not do in the product's own words.


## Evidence

- `apps/web/app/(authenticated)/settings/billing/_lib/seat-pricing.ts` — carries
  a `TODO(BUG-3330)` written when no quote endpoint existed. One exists now, so
  the comment is live rather than aspirational.
- `GET /billing/plan-prices/:planPriceId/seat-quote?seats=<n>` — returns seats,
  billable seats, unit price and total, computed by the server's single
  implementation. Guarded and tenant-scoped like every other billing read, and
  gated by the same sellability and market checks as checkout.
- `GET /billing/plan-changes/preview` and `POST /billing/plan-changes` — the
  plan-change path, with the money quoted before confirmation.
- [[BUG-3330]] and [[BUG-3331]] Resolution sections, which record both halves and
  why each stream could not do the other's work.


## Proposed Approach

No ExecPlan needed. Both contracts exist, are specced, and are described in the
two bug records; this is wiring against a settled interface.

1. Replace the browser-side seat computation with a call to the seat-quote
   endpoint, debounced on the seat input. Keep the local helper only as the
   optimistic value shown while the quote is in flight, or delete it outright if
   the latency is not worth the complexity — measure before deciding.
2. Offer an in-product plan change where a live subscription exists: show the
   previewed proration, confirm, then call the change endpoint. Keep the portal
   link as the secondary route rather than the only one.
3. Remove the `TODO(BUG-3330)` when the first lands, so it does not outlive the
   condition it describes.

Do not delete the mirrored spec fixtures until the browser stops computing the
rule. While two implementations exist, the shared fixtures are what stops them
diverging.


## Acceptance Criteria

- The plan card's order summary total comes from the server, not from
  arithmetic performed in the browser.
- A tenant on an active subscription can change plan without leaving the
  product, and sees the proration amount before confirming.
- The proration shown before confirmation is the one the payment provider
  actually charges, or is explicitly labelled an estimate where it cannot be.
- No `TODO` referencing [[BUG-3330]] remains in `seat-pricing.ts`.
- The seat-pricing rule has one implementation, or two that a test proves agree.
