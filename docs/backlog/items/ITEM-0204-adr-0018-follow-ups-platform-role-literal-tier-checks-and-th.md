# ITEM-0204 — ADR-0018 follow-ups: platform role-literal tier checks and the ungoverned legacy tenant status route

## Summary

ADR-0018 moved platform authorization to permission keys, but six places still
decide by platform role literal: `platform-runtime` `assertAdmin`, the
tenant-control-plane guard, `SuperAdminService.updatePlatformSettings`,
`LeadsService.correctAttribution` and `platform-lifecycle`
`isPlatformSuperAdmin` (Super Admin, Owner, Platform Admin), and
`platform-monitoring` `assertSuperAdmin` (Super Admin, Owner). Separately, the
legacy `PATCH /super-admin/tenants/:tenantId/status` changes tenant status
with no reason and has no UI caller, beside the governed
`POST /platform/tenants/:tenantId/status`.

## Why It Matters

None is a live defect — each is a deliberate admin-tier gate and the status
route is Super Admin only. But a role literal cannot be granted or audited like
a permission, and a second, reason-less status path is the one a future
integration will pick.

## Evidence

TASK-0032 WP-02 report, UNRESOLVED
(`docs/tasks/TASK-0032-streams/t0032-wp-02-report.md`);
`services/api/src/modules/super-admin/super-admin.controller.ts` (the
`tenants/:tenantId/status` PATCH, gated by `platform.tenants.administer`).
`correctAttribution` is kept at the admin tier deliberately (attribution moves
commission ownership) — QA summary, defect table.

## Proposed Approach

Replace each literal with a named permission key held by the same roles, one
module at a time, with a spec pinning the role-to-access outcome before and
after. Retire the legacy status route or route it through the governed
lifecycle with a required reason.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No platform authorization decision compares a role literal.
- Changing a tenant's status always records a reason and goes through one
  lifecycle path.

## Dependencies

None.

## Resolution

Fixed on `agent/backlog-0203-0204-0206`. New permission keys:
`platform.administer` (`platform.*` and PLATFORM_ADMIN) behind
`isPlatformAdminTier()`, now used by lead attribution correction, the contract
approval-step override, platform settings, the lifecycle service's admin check
and the tenant control plane guard; `leads.manage` (`leads.*` and
`platform.*`) for managing any lead; `platform.monitoring.administer`
(`platform.*`) for platform log files. Holders are unchanged, pinned by
`platform-admin-tier.spec.ts` (REG-631, QA-AUTHZ-019). The legacy
`PATCH /super-admin/tenants/:tenantId/status`, its DTO and its unused admin
proxy were removed; `POST /platform/tenants/:tenantId/status` is the one
status path. Role lists that choose notification recipients or eligible
assignees are not authorization and stay role-based.
