# ITEM-0213 — Mid-period seat increases on a Safepay subscription are not charged until renewal

## Summary

A seat increase applies immediately and rewrites `purchasedSeats`; for a Stripe subscription Stripe prorates, but for a Safepay subscription nothing is charged until the next renewal invoice, which is priced at the new seat count.

## Why It Matters

A Safepay tenant can use extra seats for up to one period without paying for them. Plan upgrades were already moved to renewal for the same reason.

## Evidence

`SeatChangeService`; `PlanChangeService.appliesImmediately`.

## Proposed Approach

Either schedule seat increases to renewal for managed providers, or issue a prorated one-off invoice payable through the existing invoice-pay path.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A mid-period seat increase on a Safepay subscription is either charged or deferred to renewal, never free.

## Dependencies

None.
