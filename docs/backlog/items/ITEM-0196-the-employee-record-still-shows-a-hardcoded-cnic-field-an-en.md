# ITEM-0196 — The employee record still shows a hardcoded CNIC field, an end-user form selector and a mislabelled Global Administrator role

## Summary

Three rows of [[ITEM-0184]] were outside the TASK-0031 work packages and remain
as found in the walkthrough:

- H9: a "CNIC" (Pakistan national id) field is hardcoded on the employee form for
  every tenant, whatever its country.
- H9: the form selector (Main / Quick form) is exposed to end users on the
  record.
- C4: the Global Administrator role is described as "Tenant owner role" while a
  tenant owner holds System Admin.

ITEM-0184 closed with its other rows done; these three move here so they are
not lost.

## Why It Matters

A country-specific identity field shown to every tenant is wrong for every
non-Pakistani customer and is a data-quality trap. An end user choosing between
forms sees a customization concept. A role description that contradicts what
the owner holds misleads anyone assigning roles.

## Evidence

- `apps/web/lib/runtime/modules/employee-metadata.adapter.ts` — `cnic` field
  (lines 235-236 at `4d249b40`) and its form placement (line 964).
- `apps/web/lib/runtime/modules/employee-metadata.adapter.ts` — form selector
  exposure (line 1051 at `df0f84f1`).
- `services/api/src/common/constants/rbac-matrix.ts` — Global Administrator
  description "Tenant owner role with full tenant security authority." (lines
  539-540 at `4d249b40`).
- `docs/tasks/TASK-0031-streams/WP-03-report.md`, ITEM-0184: "Not in this stream
  (per brief): CNIC field, form selector visibility, Global Administrator
  description (`rbac-matrix.ts`, single-writer)".

## Proposed Approach

1. Replace the hardcoded CNIC field with a national-id field whose label and
   presence come from tenant settings (country), through the existing settings
   runtime.
2. Hide the form selector from users without customization permissions.
3. Correct the Global Administrator description in `rbac-matrix.ts`, a
   single-writer file: take the lease and keep the wiring and matrix specs
   green.

No explanatory text as a remedy. No ExecPlan needed unless the national-id
setting needs a schema change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No field labelled "CNIC" appears for a tenant whose country configuration does
  not call for it.
- A user without customization permissions sees no form selector on the record.
- The Global Administrator description matches how the role is used.
