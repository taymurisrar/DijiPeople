# ITEM-0225 — Re-verify historical frontend/DTO contract-drift 400s against current DTOs before resolving them

## Summary

The production incident queue holds about 45 historical 400s, from 2026-08-10 to
2026-09-09, that look like frontend/DTO contract drift. Examples are "property X
should not exist", `pageSize must not be less than 10`, and enum and length
errors. They cover employees, departments, app-releases, attendance, leave,
payroll cycles, projects, reporting schedules, contracts, the platform-runtime
customers, leads and partners screens, and plan prices.

Some have been fixed since: the partners `pageSize` case was fixed under
[[BUG-1754]], and `reportingManagerId` has no current caller. The rest have not
been checked. Each must be checked against the current DTOs and callers before
the incidents are resolved, so that a live contract break is not closed as
history.

## Why It Matters

Under `forbidNonWhitelisted`, an unknown field is a 400. A drift that still
exists means a screen whose save fails for every user. Resolving these incidents
without checking them would hide such a screen.

## Evidence

- Production `ErrorLog`, read-only query on 2026-10-07: about 45 NEW
  `VALIDATION_FAILED` incidents with contract-shaped messages, 2026-08-10 to
  2026-09-09, none in the last 14 days.
- The triage spot-checked two of them (partners `pageSize`, and
  `reportingManagerId`), and both are no longer reachable.

## Proposed Approach

Export the distinct `(path, method, message)` set. For each, find the current
caller in `apps/web` or `apps/admin` and the current DTO, and classify it as
fixed, still live, or caller gone. Open a bug record for each live one, and
resolve the rest under [[ITEM-0222]].

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every one of the roughly 45 incidents is classified, in this item or in a
  linked record.
- Each still-live drift has its own bug record.
- The rest are resolved in the queue.

## Dependencies

None.

## Related Items

- [[ITEM-0222]]: the monitoring queue triage, which leaves these out of its bulk
  resolution.
- [[BUG-1754]]: the partners `pageSize` case.
