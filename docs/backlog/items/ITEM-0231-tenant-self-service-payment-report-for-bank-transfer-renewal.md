# ITEM-0231 — Tenant self-service payment report for bank-transfer renewal invoices

## Summary

A tenant administrator can see the payment reference and bank details of an open bank-transfer renewal invoice, but cannot tell DijiPeople "I have paid" or attach a receipt. Finance records the credit from the platform console (`recordInvoicePayment`). A tenant-side claim would shorten the loop and let finance see which invoices are probably paid.

## Why It Matters

Without it, a tenant that has paid a renewal waits for finance to notice the credit, and the first signal DijiPeople has is its own reconciliation. It never affects correctness - only a verified credit renews anything - so it is a latency and support-load cost, not a risk.

## Evidence

`services/api/src/modules/billing/services/bank-transfer.service.ts` (`recordInvoicePayment`, claim model already supports `invoiceId`); `apps/web/app/(authenticated)/settings/billing/_components/billing-settings-client.tsx` (bank details block). ADR-0029 consequences.

## Proposed Approach

A tenant-authenticated `POST /billing/invoices/:id/bank-transfer-claim` (tenant id from `request.user`, never the body) creating a `PENDING_VERIFICATION` claim with `invoiceId`, reusing the evidence validation and the operator queue. Settlement stays operator-only. No ExecPlan needed beyond EXECPLAN-0056's design.

## Acceptance Criteria

A tenant admin can submit a claim for their own open bank invoice and for no one else's; the claim appears in the queue marked as a renewal; approving it credits the invoice once; a claim from another tenant's user is refused (tested).

## Dependencies

The bank-transfer settlement work in PR #136 (EXECPLAN-0056).
