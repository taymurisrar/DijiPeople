# ITEM-0083 — Scheduled reconciliation sweep for orders stuck awaiting payment

## Summary

Part two of [[ITEM-0076]]. The operator-triggered re-check exists and is now
covered by REG-227. This is the unattended half: a job that sweeps orders left at
`PENDING_PAYMENT` past a threshold and re-checks them without anybody asking.

## Why It Matters

The operator path only helps when somebody notices. A customer whose webhook was
lost sees "We’re confirming your payment" and may never open a ticket — they are
as likely to assume the purchase failed and go away.

## Why It Is Deferred

Deliberately, and the user chose this ordering. Two reasons:

1. **It acts without a human.** A Stripe outage turns a periodic sweep into a
   retry storm against a provider that is already struggling, and every retry is
   a potential state change.
2. **The diagnosis has not been exercised yet.** `payment-diagnosis.ts` decides
   what each provider answer means, and automating a decision nobody has watched
   being made by hand is how a wrong classification becomes a wrong action at
   scale.

It should wait until the operator path has run often enough to show the
diagnosis is right.

## Proposed Approach

1. A bounded sweep — orders `PENDING_PAYMENT` for longer than N minutes and
   younger than the checkout session expiry, oldest first, capped per run.
2. Reuse `PaymentRecheckService.recheckOrder` exactly. No second path to `PAID`.
3. Back off on provider failure rather than retrying on the next tick, and stop
   entirely after consecutive failures — see [[failure-adaptation]].
4. A monitoring signal for the stuck count, so the sweep’s own health is
   visible rather than inferred.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No order reaches `PAID` other than through `confirmPayment`.
- A provider outage does not produce a retry storm; the sweep backs off and says
  so.
- The stuck-order count is observable before and after.

## Dependencies

[[ITEM-0076]] — done. Also worth having [[ITEM-0009]] first: a sweep is a
background actor, and one without observability is the hardest kind to debug.
