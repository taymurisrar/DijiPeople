# ITEM-0193 — Decide whether the hr role may manage notification events

## Summary

The new notification events page (ITEM-0180) changes two things at once for each
toggle: the tenant's channel preference and the event's notification rules. Its
write route therefore requires both `notifications.manage` and
`notifications.manageRules`. The seeded `hr` role holds `notifications.manageRules`
but not `notifications.manage`, so an HR user sees the page read-only. The
Architect did not widen the role; whether HR should manage notification events is
the owner's decision.

## Why It Matters

HR is the natural owner of leave, onboarding and payslip notifications. If HR is
meant to manage them, the new page has taken away a capability HR had on the old
rules screen. If not, the read-only page is correct and nothing changes.

## Evidence

- `services/api/src/common/constants/permissions.ts` — the `hr` role's grants
  (line 2250 at `88f33c6e`): `notifications.manageRules` without
  `notifications.manage`.
- `services/api/src/modules/notifications/notifications.controller.ts` —
  `PATCH /notifications/event-settings/:code` requires both keys.
- `docs/tasks/TASK-0031-streams/WP-05-report.md`, "Residual risks and findings
  for the orchestrator".

## Proposed Approach

Owner to choose:

1. Keep `hr` read-only on the events page (current behaviour).
2. Grant `hr` `notifications.manage`, which also lets HR change the tenant's
   channel preferences.

Option 2 is a change to the single-writer permission catalog and its seeds.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The owner's answer is recorded (ADR or decision note on this item).
- If option 2 is chosen, an HR user can toggle events and the permission
  propagation tests cover the grant.
