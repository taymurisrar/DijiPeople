# ITEM-0186 — The Form Designer palette adds a field on click only and drag-to-add does nothing

## Summary

In Customization's Form Designer, palette items can only be added to the form by
clicking them. Dragging a palette item onto the canvas does nothing, although the
designer looks like a drag-and-drop surface. Walkthrough finding C13; the save
feedback half of C13 was fixed in TASK-0031 WP-01, the drag half was not.

## Why It Matters

An administrator who drags a field and sees nothing happen concludes the designer
is broken. It is a small defect on a screen sold as usable by a business
administrator.

## Evidence

- `apps/web/app/(authenticated)/settings/customization/_components/form-designer-workspace.tsx`
  — the palette and canvas.
- `docs/tasks/TASK-0031-streams/WP-01-report.md`, "Not done / residual": "Form
  designer drag-to-add (C13) not changed."
- ITEM-0184 acceptance criterion: "either drag-to-add works or no drag
  affordance is shown".

## Proposed Approach

Either make drag-to-add place the palette item where it is dropped, with a
keyboard-equivalent path, or remove every drag affordance from the palette so
click-to-add is the evident interaction. Do not add a sentence explaining that
dragging does not work. No ExecPlan needed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Dragging a palette item onto the canvas adds it at the drop position, or the
  palette shows no drag affordance (no drag cursor, no grip, no draggable
  attribute).
- Click-to-add keeps working, with the existing success feedback on save.

## Dependencies

None.
