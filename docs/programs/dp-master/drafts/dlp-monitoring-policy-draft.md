## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to employees and contractors of [EMPLOYER NAME] who sign in to
the DijiPeople desktop agent on a company-managed device.

## 2. What may be collected

Only the capabilities your employer has switched on, and only while you are signed
in to the agent. The agent's tray menu ("What this app collects") always shows the
current list. Depending on what is switched on:

- **Clipboard content.** Text you copy may be recorded, up to [LIMIT] bytes per
  copy, when a rule your employer configured matches the source application. [FULL
  TEXT OR HASH ONLY — employer chooses per tenant.]
- **Screenshots.** A screenshot of your screen may be captured when a configured
  rule fires, for example after a copy from a spreadsheet followed by a switch to a
  messaging application. Screenshots are not captured continuously.
- **Window titles.** Off unless your employer enables them and you have
  acknowledged this policy.

The agent does not record keystrokes, the camera, the microphone or your location
under this policy.

## 4. Who can see it, and for how long

Captured content is visible only to people your employer has granted the
corresponding permission, and every view is recorded in the audit log. It is kept
for [RETENTION PERIOD] and then deleted. [Counsel to confirm.]

## 5. Your choices

You can decline to acknowledge this policy. If you decline, content capture stays
off for you, and the agent keeps working for attendance. [Employer to confirm
whether declining has employment consequences; the product does not impose any.]
You can ask [CONTACT] for access to, or deletion of, content captured about you.

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, it becomes a new version. Capture stops until you have read
and acknowledged the new version.
