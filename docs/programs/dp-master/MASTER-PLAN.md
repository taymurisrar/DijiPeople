## 1. Product vision, target customers and boundaries

DijiPeople is an industry-independent, configurable **People, Workforce & Operations Management Platform**. One shared employee identity and organizational context connects eight pillars:

- People management: HRIS, organization, talent acquisition, employment lifecycle and employee services.
- Workforce management: availability, attendance, biometric integrations, scheduling, staffing, capacity, shifts and handovers.
- Work and service management: projects, tasks, operational queues, duties, transactions, output, field/service assignments, quality and safety.
- Performance and development: goals, role-specific KPIs, feedback, appraisals, competencies, learning and career growth.
- Compensation and rewards: payroll, financial inputs, benefits, allowances, bonuses, increments, commissions and promotion cycles.
- Organizational intelligence: daily updates, decision ownership, work evidence, authentic utilization, accountability, workforce planning and management visibility.
- Workforce security: existing Windows agent, governed corporate-device activity, security posture, privacy, DLP review and access/asset lifecycle.
- Business automation: governed events/actions, workflows, business process flows (BPF), metadata-driven customization, ALM, reports, integrations and bounded AI assistance.

Target customers initially 5–250 employees (design for larger measured workloads), including clinics/labs/small hospitals, BPO/back-office/contact centers (nontechnical as well as technical), manufacturing, retail/hospitality, real estate, logistics, security, cleaning, maintenance, education, professional services, IT and general SMBs. Do not assume all employees use computers, have individual tasks, access GitHub or work fixed office hours. Do not build separate industry applications. Use configurable work profiles, units, policies, checklists, workflows, dashboards and solution packages. Customers without external systems must operate using native web forms, work queues, batch imports and supervisor verification; customers with existing systems may integrate.

**Current applications:** existing tenant web and Windows desktop agent; extend existing platform admin/landing/portal surfaces only as needed to operate these products. Browser-responsive and shared-browser/kiosk workflows allowed; no native mobile, macOS, Linux agent, separate kiosk app or empty app scaffolds now. Prepare versioned client-independent APIs, auth, attachments, offline contracts, future push abstractions, timezone/accessibility and device revocation for later mobile. Do not introduce mobile dependencies. Candidate/client portals are web surfaces, not separate app products.

**Non-goals:** full ERP/accounting, sales CRM, property listings/brokerage, manufacturing execution or inventory system, full procurement, clinical records/diagnosis/treatment, POS engine, contact-center telephony, chat/video conferencing, general antivirus/MDM, arbitrary tenant executable code, comprehensive global payroll without validated demand. Integrate existing specialized tools. Workforce-related assets, service requests, approved payroll inputs, field work and operational quality remain in scope. No covert monitoring, keylogging, continuous screen recording or personal communications collection; do not expand invasive capture. Existing sensitive DLP capabilities must be assessed and governed, not presumed safe or automatically expanded. Prioritize aggregated signals, corporate-device scope, least privilege and privacy by design.

**Commercial invariants:** exactly Starter, Growth, Enterprise predefined plans; genuinely custom customer-specific plans are allowed, no Enterprise Plus/fourth tier. Existing agreements, pricing and entitlements remain unchanged until a separately approved commercial change. Desktop agent Enterprise or explicitly entitled Custom only. Essential email branding and basic import/export remain available as agreed; honor current authoritative entitlements and distinction between branding, template customization and white label. Industry configuration cannot grant paid features. Proposed future positioning: Starter essential HR; Growth people/work/talent/scheduling/automation; Enterprise payroll, desktop, advanced intelligence/security/ALM. Explicit feature/permission mapping before each release; never silently revoke existing rights. Industry packs are not plans.

### 6.1 Universal operational model — U01–U06 (WP-02/35/06/09/36–38/30)

- **U01 — Work profiles:** configurable by role/grade/site/department/shift/project/employee, multiple effective-dated profiles per employee; knowledge, transactional, clinical, production, field, customer-facing, shift-based, management and support are starter examples, not exhaustive enums. - **U02 — Shared identity and domain ownership:** common work references and lifecycle contracts across typed domain records; project optional, department/campaign/site/station/service/customer context possible. Individual and shared assignments, distinct owner/accountability vs contributor effort. No duplicate task engine or blanket cross-domain writes. External refs scoped to tenant/integration; no raw GUID values displayed in user-facing fields. Preserve linked evidence and audit history through archive/reassignment.
- **U03 — Seven work types:** task (deliverables), transaction (calls/claims/sales), duty (coverage/rounds), output (production/inspection), service (visit/repair), event (incident/emergency), contribution (mentoring/assistance). Transaction/quantity entries may be batched/aggregated; never create a task for every checkout/call or require timesheet for each physical activity. Support routine and unplanned work, cross-department assistance, training and coverage.
- **U04 — Capture:** first-class web forms, responsive browser/shared device, supervisor verification, CSV/Excel mappings, authorized aggregate APIs and future offline synchronization contract; QR-based work confirmation optional with identity/anti-replay and verification, not proof by itself. Desktop optional for entitled corporate-device roles. Manual capture/import must work without integrations, desktop or AI; don't build native mobile now. Avoid collecting patient/customer sensitive data when aggregate operational counts suffice.
- **U05 — Quality/dependency:** configurable sampling, accepted output, rework/defects and root causes, safety checks/incident reporting, owner/deadline and evidence, equipment/material/approval/customer delays and downtime. Preserve context and report system-level constraints separately. Do not reward suppression of incident reporting or equate machinery performance to employee performance.
- **U06 — Workload digital twin (capacity model):** schedules, leave, assignments, planned tasks, other duties and actual effort; identify under/over-allocation and propose rescheduling/reallocation with human review. Not a simulation of personal behavior. Workload forecast is distinct from actual utilization.

- **A01 — Triggers:** typed record create/update/delete/assign/status/selected-field change, domain event, manual/on-demand, child workflow, calendar/scheduled/recurring, authorized webhook and resume event; stable event key, emitting module, related object, tenant, actor, correlation/causation, unique event ID, schema version, timestamp, before/after permitted values and placement. Synchronous action allowlist, bounded time/depth/work, deterministic ordering/priority, clear user-visible rejection and rollback. External email/HTTP/connector calls, waits and long-running steps forbidden in transaction; enqueue outbox atomically instead. Validate action/stage compatibility at design, publish and runtime. No transaction-local commits in nested action handlers. Security and invariant checks cannot be bypassed by workflows.

Do not directly modify financial finalization, employment status/access or protected data outside domain invariants. Map unsupported actions to explicit reasons; never silently skip and succeed.

Persist wait/timer state, version pinning, cancellation semantics and restart behavior. Compensation actions for explicitly supported side effects, not fake rollback of external systems.
- **A06 — Recipients:** per action/template/recipient group; candidate/external contact, actor, subject, record owner, assigned recruiter/interviewer/panel, hiring manager, manager/reporting chain, department head, CEO/role, team, named users, approved fixed addresses and permitted user/email fields. ### 6.4 Authentic utilization and work evidence — M01–M08 (WP-30/15/18/31)

- **M01 — Provenance:** approved time, accepted work, supervisor-confirmed duty/quantity, authorized transaction summaries, self-report, stakeholder feedback and optional desktop aggregate signals distinguished. Source record/time/range/unit, verification status, quality/context, correction and reviewer. A timesheet is approved effort, not proof of productivity; a calendar invite is not attendance; app/idle time not proof of effort. No screenshot/clipboard content used for performance scoring. Do not infer intent or classify employee as lazy.
- **M02 — Reconciliation:** dedup by tenant/source/external event and canonical contribution link, overlapping actual time intervals allocated only once; multiple corroborating sources don't add time. Multi-contributor work has explicit attribution and effort. Aggregate quantity and standard-equivalent time separate from elapsed hours. Missing, unverified, disputed and verified evidence visible; corrections append audited revisions, not silent changes. Employee can view/correct/appeal own records. Retrospective capture with configured deadline/approval.
- **M03 — Net available capacity:** effective schedule/contract minutes minus approved absences and applicable nonworking breaks/holidays (avoid double deduction). Timezone/overnight/part-time/shift rules; no valid denominator => N/A. Overtime separate, not silently capped or normalized. Allocation = committed time / net capacity; utilization = nonoverlapping recognized actual work / net capacity; billable utilization = approved/recognized billable time / net capacity. Document period, denominator, exclusions, scope, source freshness and rounding. Distinguish planned vs actual, billable vs nonbillable, downtime/blocked, unassigned capacity, missing evidence, disputed time and approved overtime. Don't call unaccounted time wasted.
- **M04 — Output/standard effort:** accepted units/quantity by type, complexity and quality; standard-equivalent effort = quantity × validated effective-dated standard per comparable type. Efficiency = validated standard-equivalent effort / comparable actual processing effort only when denominator and context valid. Don't mix types/units without defined conversion; don't force clinical, creative, emergency or strategic work into standard-time model. Example 20×5 + 10×15 + 15×4 + 2×30 = 370 standard minutes, not elapsed time. Test fixture: A 80 units/8 production hours = 10/h, B 60/5 = 12/h with 3h equipment downtime; quality/context still required. 
- **M05 — Six measurement dimensions:** availability, utilization, accepted output, quality, reliability, contributions; separate effectiveness/outcomes and management accountability. Role-specific target and evidence. Compare within meaningful scope, adjust complexity/downtime, no universal ranking or sole idle/app signal. Manager performance includes allocation quality, approvals, bottlenecks and team outcomes.
- **M06 — Role-specific metrics:** nurse duties/coverage/handovers/care safety/qualification; BPO accuracy/QA sample/SLA/schedule; factory accepted units/quality/safety/downtime; property visits/follow-up/qualified conversions/service; retail duties/POS summaries/accuracy/service; security shift coverage and incidents; developers accepted deliverables/quality/predictability/mentoring, not commit count; managers resource allocation/decision turnaround/team outcomes; recruiters hiring quality/time-to-fill/experience; HR service accuracy/compliance; consultants accepted work/stakeholder outcomes/billable hours. All examples configurable, no protected trait proxy or opaque automated employment decisions.
- **M07 — Analytics:** evidence coverage/uncertainty and sample size; role/time/team/organization/project filters; metric definitions with unit/formula/source/aggregation/version and effective dates; drillthrough to allowed records, snapshot finalized periods, reproducible totals, safe exports. Human reviewer contextualizes metrics, employees contest inaccurate data. Enforce data minimization, retention and small-group privacy where applicable.
- **M08 — Cost and forecast:** confidential internal rates and billable rates, budget/retainer consumption, labor cost by work/site/campaign/project, approved timesheet and payroll inputs, gross margin assumptions, utilization/coverage demand forecast, resource/skill allocation. Scenario simulation is advisory; no automatic staffing/pay changes. Use accurate Decimal arithmetic and data provenance, not invented inputs.

### 9.2 Autonomous bounded execution protocol

1. Pick next dependency-ready slice, check actual changes from other agents and integration head, obtain lease, record plan and expected tests. If audit finds existing feature meets criteria, record evidence and reuse; do not rewrite for novelty. If partial, fill gaps only. 
2. Implement end to end; run targeted checks; independent review according to repo. Fix regressions in same slice, not separate owner prompt. Root-cause diagnose before changing; keep a compact record of failed approaches and evidence. 
3. If same failure persists after **two materially different evidence-based remediation attempts**, stop repeating that approach. Do not impose a global two-fix limit on real implementation; use this bound to prevent repeated blind retries. 4. Integrate small changes through repository-approved branch/PR/CI mechanism. No force-push/reset/deleting unrelated branches, no weakening tests, no editing `main`. Do not automatically merge if repo policy or permissions require review. Record exact SHA and evidence. 
5. Advance state and continue next ready work automatically as resources/session allow. If dependency blocked, switch to independent slices. If context/session ends, persist all progress and return compact handoff, not a new full plan. Resume from evidence and actual state next session.
