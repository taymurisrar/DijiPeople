# TASK-0038 — Complete multi-payment billing end-to-end

## Objective

Deliver a server-controlled multi-payment architecture and a complete Pakistani
bank-transfer purchase path: an email-verified buyer receives a real frozen
invoice and unique reference, an authorized platform operator independently
verifies funds, and only that atomic verification activates the subscription and
existing provisioning workflow. Stripe and Safepay remain isolated and hidden
unless market, environment, approval, credentials, and readiness all pass.
